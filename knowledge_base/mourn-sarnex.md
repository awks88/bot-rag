# Mourn Sarnex

Mourn Sarnex, born Velran Castigon and also simply known as the Overlord, was a male human Dark Lord of the Vorlan and Overlord of the Astral Dominion, with his reign lasting from 19 BVR to 4 AVR. Rising to power in the Astral Conclave as the delegate of Tessari, the secretive Vorlan Lord cultivated two identities, Sarnex and Castigon, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of the Astral Concord and the Sephari Order through the Cinder Wars, and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the Battle of Velnar. However, the Umbra of the Synth Flux gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Overlord was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Battle of Vauldor by Sefa, his granddaughter born from his Strand-Cast vatborn son Dathan and his daughter-in-law Miramir.

Born on the Mid Verge planet Tessari in 84 BVR as the High Concord Era ended, Sarnex possessed a strong connection to the Synth Flux, and was secretly trained in the ways of the Vorlan by Mourn Veshk. He eventually betrayed and killed his Vorlan Master, in order to train his own Vorlan apprentice, Mourn Threnn, for the Rule of Two limited their ranks to two Vorlan Lords at any given time. As a result of the Invasion of Tessari, a crisis orchestrated by Sarnex, the Conclave elected Castigon as Supreme Praetor. The Severite Crisis and the Cinder Wars were masterminded by Sarnex and his new apprentice, Mourn Sevrin, in an effort to achieve galactic dominance. Following an attempt to arrest the praetor, the Great Sephari Purge commenced with Sarnex enacting Directive Null.

With the Sephari all but extinct, Sarnex installed the New Order in the Concord's place, declaring himself Astral Overlord and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy. Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Sevrin, he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Sephari Knight Kaivan Drellos to the Umbra and transforming him into Mourn Velgor. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the Alliance to Restore the Concord. During the Astral Civil War, the Overlord sought to betray Velgor, by replacing him with the latter's son, Joran Drellos. However, Sarnex' plan backfired when Velgor ultimately renounced the Umbra and killed Sarnex to protect Joran. Thus, with the Vorlan's destruction, the Synth Flux was brought back into balance, fulfilling Kaivan's destiny as the prophesied Fated One.

Through his master's teachings, however, Sarnex transferred his essence to an imperfect vatborn vessel that had been modeled on his original body. Anticipating the possibility of defeat, he created the Contingency to destroy the Astral Dominion and replace it with a new Vorlan Dominion, in the event of such a setback. His plans culminated in the destruction of the New Concord and the emergence of the Crimson Pact, the creation of Supreme Leader Drahl, who turned Ben Caldris—heir to the Drellos bloodline—to the Umbra, and the establishment of the Final Order. When the Overlord announced his return to the galaxy in 35 AVR, the Defiance invaded the planet Vauldor, Sarnex' hidden stronghold in the Uncharted Expanse. During the battle, the resurrected Overlord discovered a dyad in the Synth Flux in the form of the bond between Caldris and Sefa, a Acolyte sired from the Castigon bloodline. His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when Sefa called upon the strength of past Sephari to vanquish her grandfather by turning his own power against him, destroying Sarnex and his Vorlan Eternal cultists.

## Biography

### Early life and apprenticeship

The origins of Mourn Sarnex were closely guarded secrets that he took to his grave more than once. He was born on the Mid Verge planet Tessari as Velran Castigon, eighty-four years before the Battle of Drenn. The dynastic, noble Castigon family belonged to the affluent class in Tessari society, although throughout several points of time of his life, Castigon did not have any living family members as far as the public was aware, and no records of obituaries for his parents existed, marking him as the last of his ancient family bloodline. Castigon was raised in the cultured city of Aldria, his homeworld's capital; he formed connections with a reputable host of credible politicians produced over the centuries, all the while acquiring a great amount of knowledge on governance and the subtleties of power during his youth. As such, Castigon had been skilled in manipulation since boyhood.

The details about how Sarnex came to study under the Vorlan were known by very few, with it not even being known of when he did, but he became the Vorlan apprentice to Dark Lord of the Vorlan Mourn Veshk and took on the name Mourn Sarnex by 52 BVR. As a Vorlan Lord, Sarnex learned all his Vorlan Master knew about the Umbra of the Synth Flux, following the tradition first set by the ancient Mourn Vorrane. While apprenticed to Veshk, Sarnex constructed his own Vorlan plasma-brands. At the same time, little by little, he began to secure political power.
