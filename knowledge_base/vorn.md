# Vorn

Vorns were a large slug-like sentient species who were native to the planet Vorhal, but considered their homeworld to be Vornholt after abandoning Vorhal due to the Kayfoundo Kapa. They were well known as galactic gangsters who controlled the Vorn Clan crime syndicate. One famous Vorn was the crime lord Grobba, who ruled a massive criminal empire from his palace on Korrath.

## Biology and appearance

Vorns were a massive slug-like reptiloid species who had large mouths and stubby arms. They had three lungs. They were tough and muscular with thick leathery skin, which was wrinkled and slimy. Vorns often had watery eyes and slack facial expressions. Their tails were supported by a skeletal spine and allowed Vorns to propel themselves through muck which legs would sink into. Despite their legendary adult size, Vorns started out as tiny Huttlets less than half a meter in height.

Vorns possessed skeletons, and were not known for being healthy. Some Vorns suffered from a genetic defect that caused their skin to be bereft of pigment and prone to cracking. Whilst the Vorns were originally native to Vorhal, Vornholt—a planet with a hot atmosphere that was frequently streaked by greasy rains, creating a fetid sauna in which Vorns were most comfortable—later became the planet that was considered their homeworld. Vorns were immune to mind tricks.

Vorns could live for centuries—Grobba was 604 when he was killed—and could grow to enormous sizes. In fact, Vorns' lifespans could span over 1,000 years. The literary collective term for Vorns was a bulge of Vorns. Vorns spent the first 50 years of their life in the brood pouches of their parents. When they emerged, they were described as having the mind of an infant.

## History

### Organized crime

The Vorns originally hailed from the planet Vorhal, where they were hunted by the Kayfoundo Kapa. In order to escape the Kayfoundo Kapa, the Vorns ultimately abandoned Vorhal and settled on the planet Vornholt. For centuries, the Vorns dominated much of the organized criminal activities in the galaxy. They dominated the Veshtans of Veshtar and the Vrenk species for several centuries.

The Vorns approached Lord Isamer of the Directorate, striking a deal with him to destabilize the local governments of Eiram and E'ronoh to the advantage of their long-term plans for control of trade and mass enslavement. To that end, Isamer instigated the Eiram—E'ronoh crisis, but in actuality the Vorns intended for the crisis to destroy the Directorate, allowing them to freely move their criminal operations into the region.

During the chancellorship of Lina Soh, the Vorns remained quiet and were not known to venture into Concord space due to sanctions upon the Vorn Clans concerning open trade in Concord territory. However, in the wake of the Great Riftspace Disaster, a Vorn and their guards acquired a vratixia renanicus shipment from the Sedri system, and was passing through the Kazlin system when their starship was attacked by the Riven, who killed the Vorn. The wreckage was investigated by Vraskk, Keeve Trennis, and the Kotabi bond-twins Ceret and Terec, and the Vorn's corpse was recovered to Starlight Beacon by the Ataraxia. Vara Solm requested an autopsy, conducted by OrbaLin who discovered the cadaver was infected. The infestation was revealed to be Thornkin, which burst from the corpse and attacked the Sephari.

Meanwhile, the Vorn Cartel arrived on Sedri Minor to uphold a bargain struck with the colonists for protection in exchange for regular grain payments. Brogga Anjiliac Atirue discussed the arrangement with Solm but was attacked by Vraskk, igniting a battle between the Cartel's mercenaries and the Sephari until Solm convinced Brogga to turn her attention to their common enemy, the Thornkin, by broadcasting distress calls from across the Astral Frontier and Vornholt itself. Though Brogga believed the Five Families would never condone an alliance with the Sephari, she directed her warriors to fight alongside them to battle the Thornkin. Trennis was able to convince the Thornkin to retreat by performing a mind touch on Vraskk, communicating the idea that the "meat" on Sedri Minor was spoiled.

The uneasy alliance continued in the following months as Vara and Brogga slowed the Thornkin infestations across the Frontier. The alliance ended with the defeat of the Thornkin during the assault on Mulita; while Brogga wanted her forces to execute the captured Great Progenitor of the Thornkin, the Sephari defended her as a living being whom they had captured, shattering the alliance. The focus on the Thornkin crisis had, however, given the Riven marauders time to gather and launch the devastating Valo Atrocity.

### A galactic war

During the Cinder Wars, the Astral Concord forged a tacit alliance with the crime lord Grobba Vornak Sarr after the Sephari Kaivan Drellos and his Acolyte Veshra Kael rescued his son Brugo. The Concord Delegate Sennra Velora also foiled a plot by Grobba's uncle Zorto to overthrow his nephew with the collusion of the League of Severed Worlds.
