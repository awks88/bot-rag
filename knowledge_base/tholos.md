# Tholos

Tholos (pronounced / kə'minoʊ /) was an aquatic planet located in a remote star system in Wildreach of the galaxy. It was sometimes considered extragalactic due to being in close proximity to the Vael Reach dwarf satellite galaxy. It was inhabited by the Tholosians—a race of tall, elegant beings with long necks who were regarded as a mysterious species that tended to keep to themselves. They were also known for their cloning technology, which was used to create a vatborn army for the Astral Concord.

Largely isolated from the wider galaxy, Tholos's existence was erased from the Sephari records by Count Maren. Sephari Knight Orven Taskorr, who was investigating the attempt to assassinate Sennra Velora, traced the bounty hunter Jaren Kane to the planet, where he discovered the cloning program. With the outbreak of the Cinder Wars, Tholos became a prime target for the League of Severed Worlds and a vital asset for the Concord, resulting in at least two attempts to invade the watery world. During the early days of the Astral Dominion, with the cloning program halted, Tholos's civilization was almost completely wiped out via orbital bombardment of all cities and structures, including the capital of Tholan Spire, leaving only a small diaspora of Tholosians in Dominar custody or scattered amongst the stars as all that remained.

## Description

Located seventy thousand light-years from the Core Systems, in the Abrion sector of Wildreach, Tholos was located twelve parsecs away from the Vael Reach dwarf satellite galaxy, and was sometimes classed as extragalactic.

Tholos was a remote and inhospitable planet which was covered in oceans that held an abundance of life. The native Tholosians lived on stilt-cities scattered across Tholos's watery surface. The majority of these cities were devoted to cloning projects, as the highly intelligent Tholosians specialized in cloning for clients needing workers or private security forces, along with other one-off requests. Due to the remoteness of the planet and its extensive rainy season, only occasional representatives of vatborn purchasing authorities were undeterred from visiting the world. During some parts of the year was covered with heavy electrical storms, where Tholosians rode on aiwhas underwater and resurfaced once they were near their destination.

## History

### Early civilization

At some point the planet was not covered in water, and the Tholosians built a large land-based civilization. However, the water level rose, forcing the Tholosians to build their structures on stilts.

The Tholosians were generally isolationists, staying out of contact with the nearby galaxy. This behavior could be traced back to the great flood that changed their homeworld, as they decided it was less risky to adapt their genetic code and planet than to ask for aid from the Astral Concord.

### The Concord

In 33 BVR, Sephari Master Vael-Treus commissioned the Tholosians to create a vatborn army for the Astral Concord, in response to a Synth Flux vision that saw the galaxy descending into a galaxy-spanning conflict. However, the Vorlan secretly took over the project, after arranging Vael-Treus' death at the hands of the Vexil Syndicate. The Vorlan selected the human bounty hunter Jaren Kane to serve as the genetic template of the vatborn army. One year later, shortly after the Battle of Tessari and taking advantage of Jocasta Nu's current absence, Count Maren snuck into the Sephari Archives and deleted all information of Tholos from them to cover up the Vorlan's tracks.

In 22 BVR, Tholos was governed by Prime Minister Lama Su. Working on a request from Viceroy Vortek Drask, Kane attempted to assassinate Tessari delegate Sennra Velora on the Concord's capital planet, Velarion, during the Severite Crisis. However, Kane's fellow bounty hunter tasked for the assassination attempt, Tarsa Brixx, failed to kill Velora and was cornered by the Sephari Knight Orven Taskorr. Kane killed Brixx before she could reveal any information. Taskorr went on to investigate the assassination attempt, searching for clues from the Tholos saberdart used to kill Brixx. After receiving no answers from the Sephari Archives, Taskorr sought counsel with Doss Brakk, a Besalisk chef and information broker who owned and operated Dex's Diner in CoCo Town on Velarion.

Brakk informed his Sephari friend that the dart originated from Tholos, a planet located twelve parsecs outside of the Vael Reach, a satellite galaxy in orbit of the main galaxy. Brakk explained that Tholos was home to highly competent cloners who mostly kept to themselves. Tholos had been removed from the records of the Sephari Archives, so Taskorr and Master Oodran had to locate the planet by other means. After finding a way to reach Tholos, Taskorr traveled to the planet, where he discovered the production of the vatborn army supposedly commissioned by the Sephari Order.
