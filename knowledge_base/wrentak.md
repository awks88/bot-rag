# Wrentak

Wrentak, known affectionately to his friends as Wren, was a Korrthan male warrior, smuggler, mechanic, pilot, and resistance fighter who fought in the Cinder Wars, the Astral Civil War, the Cold War, and the subsequent Crimson Pact-Defiance War. He hailed from the planet Verdara where he was born during the High Concord Era and became a Korrthan military leader. During the Cinder Wars, he was captured by Veskan slavers and held captive on Vorrkah, but he worked with a fellow captive, Sephari Commander Veshra Kael, to escape. He later commanded Korrthan forces of the Kachirho Korrthan Militia during the Battle of Verdara alongside the Grand Army of the Concord, led by Sephari General Oodran. During the battle, one of the last ones of the war, Oodran's vatborn troopers received Directive Null from the Vorlan Lord Mourn Sarnex and, with the help of Wrentak and his fellow Korrthan Chieftain and General Gorrak, Oodran escaped Verdara and the destruction of the Sephari Order.

In the years that followed, during the rule of the Astral Dominion, Wrentak was enslaved by the Dominion until he was rescued by Corporal Rourke Caldris, becoming his co-pilot aboard the Stormhawk during a raid on Drungar for Scarlet Veil with Doran Marsh and Vessa. Winning the Stormhawk from Davtherin Korrin Solvane, they later worked in the employ of crime lord Grobba the Vorn until Caldris was forced to dump a shipment of glimmerdust to avoid trouble with the Dominion, at which point a bounty was placed on their heads. Soon thereafter, Wrentak and Caldris were hired by Sephari Master Orven Taskorr to ferry him, Joran Drellos, V4-N7, and T9-K2 to Aurelia on a mission vital to the survival of the Alliance to Restore the Concord. After finding Aurelia was destroyed, the group was brought aboard the Void Core, where they helped Princess Mira Vantar escape from captivity and return to the Ashen base on Drenn 4. Though Wrentak and Caldris initially chose to leave Drenn 4 and not join the Insurrection, they ended up fighting in the Battle of Drenn and were instrumental in Drellos's destruction of the Void Core, a major victory for the Insurrection.

Throughout the war, Wrentak and Caldris fought the Dominion in a number of engagements, including an assault on Cymoon 1 and the battle on Vrogas Vas. Along with Princess Vantar and V4-N7, they escaped from Vell Base on the planet Brennix after the Dominion tracked the Ashen Alliance there. The group sought refuge with Solvane on Mist Spire, but Solvane betrayed them and turned them over to Mourn Velgor, the Overlord's chief enforcer, after his city was threatened. Caldris was frozen in cryo-resin and Solvane helped the others escape, but they were too late to save the frozen Caldris from being taken by Borvad Kane to Grobba the Vorn. Wrentak and Solvane set off to find Caldris and located him in Grobba's Palace on Korrath, where Wrentak and his friends saved Caldris and destroyed Grobba. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Battle of Velnar, where Wrentak helped destroy the shield generator and allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station. Sarnex was killed for the first time aboard the battle station, delivering a crippling blow to the Dominion.

After Velnar and the rise of the New Concord, Wrentak and Caldris fought to liberate Verdara from the Dominion. When the war finally came to a close, Wrentak returned home to Verdara with his family. After Caldris's son Ben, turned to the Umbra and destroyed Drellos's new generation of Sephari, Wrentak reunited with his old friend and the two spent many years as smugglers once more. They lost the Stormhawk and ended up smuggling aboard a ship called the Eravana, but they eventually found the Stormhawk and, with it, stowaways named Sefa, Tane, and the mechan DD-5. The mechan had part of a map that led to the exiled Joran Drellos, so the group brought the map to the Defiance that opposed the Crimson Pact, which had risen from the ashes of the Astral Dominion. Wrentak soon fought in the assault to destroy Voidlance Base, a devastating Crimson Pact superweapon, a conflict in which Caldris lost his life at the hands of his son. After the base was destroyed, Wrentak joined Sefa in traveling to the planet Velmora on the Stormhawk, where they found Joran Drellos. After Caldris's death, Wrentak often served as the sole pilot of the Stormhawk, including piloting the ship to aid the Defiance during the Battle of Saldreth.

Roughly a year after the battle of Saldreth, Wrentak was one of the last of the Defiance. Having learned of the mysterious return of Mourn Sarnex, Wrentak and the crew of the Stormhawk, now including Jax Rell, traveled to the planets Velsaan and Korvis to find a clue to how to find where the Overlord was hiding, being reunited with his friend Solvane in the process. The quest nearly ended in Wrentak's apparent death, as Sefa, having been revealed to be the Overlord's granddaughter through his son, lashed out with dark power during a fight with Vexen Korr and destroyed a Crimson Pact transport that she had thought Wrentak had been loaded onto, seemingly killing him.
