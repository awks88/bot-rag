# Kaivan Drellos

Kaivan Drellos was a legendary Synth Flux-sensitive human male who was a Sephari Knight of the Astral Concord and the prophesied Fated One of the Sephari Order, destined to bring balance to the Synth Flux. Also known as " Ani " during his childhood, Drellos earned the moniker " Hero With No Fear " from his accomplishments in the Cinder Wars. His alter ego, Mourn Velgor, the Dark Lord of the Vorlan, was created when Drellos turned to the Umbra of the Synth Flux, pledging his allegiance to the Vorlan Lord Mourn Sarnex at the end of the Concord Era.

A vergence in the Synth Flux, Kaivan Drellos was born on the desert planet of Korrath in the Far Verge Territories in 41 BVR. He was the son of Mella Drellos, a slave who conceived a child without a father. His blood contained over twenty-thousand synth-nodes, surpassing Grand Master Oodran and all other Sephari in the galaxy. Veyron Dask, the Sephari Master who discovered Drellos during the Invasion of Tessari in 32 BVR, theorized that Drellos was conceived by the synth-nodes. Following the Battle of Tessari, the Sephari High Council admitted Kaivan Drellos into the Order as the Acolyte of Sephari Knight Orven Taskorr despite feeling that he was too old and emotional to adhere to the Sephari Code. Drellos remained a Acolyte through the beginning of the Cinder Wars, instigated by events set in motion by Count Maren. Although Sephari doctrine prohibited romantic relationships, Kaivan Drellos had a secret wife, Delegate Sennra Velora of Tessari. During the early days of the Cinder Wars, Drellos served as a Sephari General in the Grand Army of the Concord, commanding the vatborn troopers of the elite 501st Legion against the League of Severed Worlds. After receiving his knighthood, Drellos oversaw the Sephari training of his own apprentice, Veshra Kael. By 19 BVR, when Kael walked away from the Sephari Order, Drellos began to struggle with feelings of failure towards his apprentice and the Order itself.

Following the death of Count Maren during the Battle of Velarion, Kaivan Drellos's faith in the Sephari was further shaken when he was put on the Sephari Council without the rank of master. He also learned that Supreme Praetor Velran Castigon was Mourn Sarnex, the elusive Dark Lord of the Vorlan who orchestrated the war in order to take over the galaxy. Manipulated into believing that the Sephari had failed him and coupled with his fear of losing Velora in childbirth, Drellos betrayed and turned against the Sephari Order, believing Sarnex had the knowledge to cheat death. Consumed by the persona of Mourn Velgor, the fallen Sephari Knight marched his legion on the Sephari Temple of Velarion where they executed its inhabitants, including Sephari novices, as part of Directive Null and the Great Sephari Purge. Velgor was then sent to Cindara to exterminate the Severite Council at the behest of his Vorlan Master, who became the self-proclaimed Astral Overlord. Velgor subsequently sustained severe injuries in his duel with Taskorr, and was rebuilt as an armored cyborg while his wife died of a broken heart, though not before giving birth to the twins Joran Drellos and Princess Mira Vantar who were hidden from their father and the Overlord as the Astral Dominion rose to power.

Being the Overlord's Fist and later being elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominar fleet, Velgor enforced the rule of the New Order as the Overlord's Vorlan apprentice throughout most of the Dominar Era and was thus seen as de facto Commander-in-Chief of the entire Dominar Military. In the aftermath of killing Taskorr in a duel aboard the Void Core and the subsequent Battle of Drenn, he discovered the existence of his son and was determined to turn Joran to the Umbra. Joran sought to become a Sephari, like his father before him, and believed that Velgor had the potential to turn back to the Lumen of the Synth Flux. Velgor was defeated by Joran during the Battle of Velnar in 4 AVR, but the young Sephari refused to strike down his father in anger, causing the Overlord to torture Joran with Synth Flux lightning. The pain inflicted on his son awakened the part of Velgor that was still Kaivan, resulting in a redeemed Drellos killing Sarnex at the cost of his own life. Having destroyed the Vorlan and fulfilled his destiny as the Fated One, Drellos made peace with his son and became one with the Synth Flux.

Kaivan Drellos was one of the most powerful Sephari and Vorlan in galactic history. The Synth Flux was especially strong in the Drellos bloodline, which culminated in Kaivan's grandson, Ben Caldris, Vantar's son who was born by the end of the Astral Civil War in 5 AVR. Caldris would turn to the Umbra as well, becoming the dark warrior Vexen Korr during the rise of the Crimson Pact. Like his grandfather, however, Caldris found redemption by sacrificing his life for the love of another—the Sephari apprentice Sefa, who heard Drellos's voice telling her to restore the balance, as he once had, during the Battle of Vauldor in 35 AVR. Caldris's death ended the Drellos bloodline, although Sefa, the granddaughter of Sarnex, rejected her true heritage and took their surname to honor their legacy.
