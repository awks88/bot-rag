# Maren

Maren was a Synth Flux-sensitive human male Serennian who served as a Sephari Master during the waning years of the Astral Concord and later Count of House Valdoris. He became dissatisfied with the Sephari Order and fell to the Umbra of the Synth Flux, becoming the Dark Lord of the Vorlan Mourn Sevrin. After leaving the Sephari Order, he served as Count of Valdoris and Head of State of the League of Severed Worlds during the Cinder Wars. He was the second apprentice of Mourn Sarnex, the Dark Lord of the Vorlan, whose plan to conquer the galaxy relied on Maren leading a pan- galactic secessionist movement against the Concord. As such, Maren immersed himself in the Umbra of the Synth Flux and worked tirelessly to advance his and his master's Grand Plan while being unaware that he was merely a pawn of his master.

Born in 102 BVR during the High Concord Era, Maren was taken by the Sephari Order after being abandoned by his family and learned the Sephari arts as the Acolyte of Oodran, the legendary Grand Master of the Sephari Order; he went on to take Voss Tarmin and the maverick Veyron Dask as his two Acolyte learners at different points, before eventually ascending to the Sephari Council. He went on to become a respected and legendary Sephari Master in his own right. A political idealist, the corruption in the Astral Conclave—as well as the traditions of the Sephari—disillusioned him. In 42 BVR Maren left the Order and returned to his homeworld, where he reclaimed his title of Count and heritage as a nobleman after overthrowing his corrupt brother, Ramil. His former peers believed his decision was misguided but still held him in high esteem. However the Count of Valdoris began to secretly conspire with Mourn Sarnex. After the death of Veyron Dask at the Battle of Tessari in 32 BVR, and following the duel in the Works which saw the death of Sephari Master Vossa, Maren embraced the Umbra and adopted the name of Mourn Sevrin upon fully becoming the apprentice of Mourn Sarnex. Throughout the first decade of Velran Castigon's chancellery, Maren used his resources and charisma to recruit entire star systems to the Severite cause. At the same time, he oversaw the development of two vast armies that would be pitted against each other in the conflict to come: the Severite Mechan Army, which consisted of battle mechans manufactured on worlds such as Karnak; and the Grand Army of the Concord, composed of vatborn troopers bred on Tholos and modeled on the bounty hunter Jaren Kane.

After years of maneuvering the galaxy into a state of war, the Cinder Wars began when the newly formed Concord Military invaded Karnak in 22 BVR. As leader of the Severites, Maren delegated military authority to a cadre of subordinates personally trained by himself, including General Skarl and Nyssa Vashka. While Skarl was trained only in the art of plasma-brand combat, Vashka was strong with the Synth Flux, affording her the opportunity to learn the ways of the Umbra from Maren. The Vorlan, however, remained faithful to Mourn Vorrane's Rule of Two; and as such, Maren was compelled to betray his disciple after Sarnex became suspicious of his own apprentice's intentions. Though he sought to replace Vashka with Vrenn Garokk and later Quinlan Salin, Maren ultimately remained beholden to his master's will, which would eventually be his undoing.

By the third year of the war, Maren and Skarl succeeded in abducting Praetor Castigon (per Sarnex' plan) from the Concord capital of Velarion in 19 BVR. During the ensuing Battle of Velarion, he was confronted and defeated by the Sephari Knight Kaivan Drellos, who summarily executed the Count via beheading at the Praetor's behest. In the days following Maren's death, Castigon revealed to the Sephari that he was, in fact, Mourn Sarnex, who betrayed his apprentice in order to replace him with the younger and more promising Drellos. With the subsequent fall of the Sephari Order, Sarnex consolidated his power upon declaring himself Overlord of the Astral Dominion, which Sevrin had an instrumental role in establishing. As for Drellos, he succeeded the late Mourn Sevrin as Sarnex' third and last apprentice, resulting in his transformation into Mourn Velgor and proving his slain victim's words of sensing fear, hate, and anger.

## Biography

### Early life

Maren was born in 102 BVR during the High Concord Era, on the planet Valdoris to the planet's ruler, Count Valdor, and his wife, Countess Anya. The child's Synth Flux-sensitivity manifested itself rapidly, scaring his father, who hated and feared those he dismissed as "freaks." Valdor contacted the Sephari Order and told them to take his son. However, instead of waiting for the Sephari seekers to arrive, Valdor abandoned the infant outside his palace on the edges of the forest without clothes or any form of identification. A seeker rescued Maren before he froze to death or was eaten by spine-wolves, and brought the child to the Sephari Grand Temple on Velarion.
