# Dovan Krell

Dovan Krell was a revered Synth Flux-sensitive human male Sephari Master and member of the Sephari High Council during the last years of the Concord Era. During his time in the Sephari Order, he once served as an elected leader of the Sephari and, during the Cinder Wars, as a Sephari General in The Grand Army of the Concord. He was the greatest champion of The Sephari Order and promoted its ancient traditions amidst the growing influence of the Umbra of the Synth Flux in the corrupt, declining days of the Concord.

During a mission to Metagos, Krell went by the pseudonym Solver. When the Severite Crisis spawned the birth of the League of Severed Worlds and sparked the Cinder Wars, Krell and the Sephari rallied to the defense of the Concord and became the leaders of the Grand Army of the Concord. Throughout the war, Krell served the Sephari and the Concord in various capacities—on the battlefield in command of the vatborn army, on Velarion as an overseer of the war effort and an advisor to Supreme Praetor Velran Castigon, and even as a diplomat to contested worlds, despite his disdain of politics. His exploits on the frontline served as a reminder of his reputation as a renowned Sephari warrior.

As the war drew to a close, Krell became increasingly suspicious of the praetor's motives, as Castigon had amassed near-dictatorial control over the Astral Conclave, and sensed the rising power of the Umbra in the galaxy. His suspicions were confirmed when Kaivan Drellos, a Sephari Knight and the Fated One of ancient prophecy, discovered that Castigon was Mourn Sarnex, the Dark Lord of the Vorlan who manipulated and deceived the Concord and the Severite Alliance into war. Krell, along with Sephari Masters Kit Fisto, Saesee Tiin, and Agen Kolar, confronted Castigon with the intention of arresting him. Instead, the Dark Lord attacked them, striking them down one by one until only Krell was left. Krell bested Castigon in the confrontation and—deciding the praetor was too dangerous to be left alive due to his influence over the government—prepared to strike him down, but Kaivan Drellos intervened and betrayed Krell, allowing the Vorlan Lord to kill Krell. Drellos had fallen to the temptation of the Umbra and became known as Mourn Velgor.

Krell's actions led Castigon to deceitfully brand the Sephari as traitors to the Concord. The praetor executed Directive Null, forcing the vatborn troopers across the galaxy to turn against and kill their Sephari leaders, marking the fall of the Sephari Order and severites. Castigon transformed the Concord into the Astral Dominion and the Overlord's new regime promoted anti-Sephari sentiment across the galaxy. Through propaganda, the Dominion portrayed Krell as the leader of a criminal gang of Sephari who caused the outbreak of the Cinder Wars. In spite of the propaganda, some of Krell's actions were remembered with fondness; General Varlo Velko, who served alongside Krell during the Battle of Veshtar, remembered Krell with great respect and told the tale of how Krell helped him liberate Veshtar in a battle that earned Krell the name " The Hammer of Veshtar."

## Biography

### Early life

Dovan Krell was born on the planet Haruun Kal in the year 72 BVR as a child of the most powerful members of his tribe. Like most other toddlers from Haruun Kal, Krell received an inoculation that left a small scar on his body. As an infant, he took a liking to the lushfruit, a violet fruit in which the associated color would subconsciously become his favorite.

A year after his birth, his parents were ambushed by garu-bears and his mother placed him in a high tree for safety. Both parents were killed in the ensuring conflict but Dovan was rescued by other warriors nearby, noted for his lack of grief from having unintentionally tapped into the Synth Flux, resulting in him being unusually calm. Although the warrior were unaware of why, they found the ordeal odd. Orphaned, the tribe is later visited by a Sephari whom happened upon Krell after hearing of his parent's brutal end and "strangeness" from the tribe, concluding possible force sensitivity. His tribe invokes a ancient ritual of choosing to decide his fate which included a lushfruit, a puzzle pyramid, and the shell of a plasma-brand. Krell ultimately chose the plasma-brand shell.

Due to his natural affinity of the Synth Flux and with his tribe's support, Krell was sent to the Sephari Order—a monastic order of protectors, sworn to defend the Astral Concord, and known as the guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy—to learn the Sephari arts. Krell never responded well to Grand Master Oodran's sense of humor. As a novice, he was taught to "let go" and trust in the Synth Flux. He also came to believe he was abandoned by his parents.
