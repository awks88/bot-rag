# T9-K2

T9-K2, pronounced Tee-Nine-Deetoo and often referred to as R2 (Tee-Nine), was an R2-series astromech mechan manufactured by Cogwright Industries with masculine programming. A smart, spunky mechan who served a multitude of leaders, T9-K2 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in galactic history, his bravery and ingenuity saved the galaxy on numerous occasions.

Beginning his service in the employ of Queen Velora of Tessari, T9-K2 wound up serving Sephari Knight Kaivan Drellos during the waning years of the Astral Concord, often accompanied by the protocol mechan V4-N7 in many adventures throughout the Cinder Wars. After Drellos turned to the Umbra of the Synth Flux, the mechan served Delegate Daro Vantar for a time in the Dominar Conclave. Around nineteen years following the purge of the Astral Concord, T9-K2 played a pivotal role in helping the Ashen Alliance destroy the Dominion's Void Core superweapon. He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving Sephari Knight Joran Drellos, the son of his two former masters throughout the Astral Civil War, he participated in both the Battles of Brennix and Velnar, and witnessed the successful destruction of the Dominion's second Void Core four years after the first one was demolished.

In the subsequent thirty years, R2 accompanied Joran Drellos until the undoing of his attempted resurrection of the Sephari Order in 28 AVR. Following Joran's disappearance, R2 entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer mechan models started to out-compute the aging astromech. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Insurrection protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old mechans, allowing him a semi-retirement in the Defiance as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.

Following his reawakening after the Battle of Voidlance Base, R2 went on to accompany the Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger Sefa on her journey to train under Joran Drellos on Velmora, the planet of the First Sephari Temple, where the mechan was reunited with his old master. T9-K2 continued to aid the Defiance alongside his longtime counterpart V4-N7 throughout the Crimson Pact-Defiance War, culminating in a decisive victory at the Battle of Vauldor, in which he was an active participant in the role of copilot for General Jax Rell.

## Biography

### Serving Sennra Velora

T9-K2 was built around prior or during 32 BVR. He was one of several astromech mechans on Queen Sennra Velora's Tessari Royal Starship during the invasion of Tessari by the Commerce League that year. The ship was used by Queen Velora and her entourage in an attempt to pass the blockade and escape the planet. Unluckily for the passengers, the vessel took a hit to the shield generator from a Commerce League battleship. The four astromechs were sent out to repair the ship, but only T9-K2 survived—the rest were pulverized by stray pulse-caster fire. T9-K2 was able to bring the shields online by bypassing the main power drive, allowing the ship to narrowly escape into riftspace.

As the ship traveled to Korrath, T9-K2 was taken before Daxa, the Queen's decoy in the guise of Velora, where he was commended for his actions. The ship landed on Korrath without any further incidents, although the voyage fried the riftdrive. T9-K2 accompanied Sephari Master Veyron Dask, Naari exile Bimo Trask, and Sennra, the queen in disguise, to the city of Krevar Hollow to find a replacement. In the junk shop of the Brunni merchant Grezzo they found they could not afford a replacement riftdrive and later took shelter from a sandstorm in the home of Grezzo's slaves, a boy named Kaivan Drellos, and his mother, Mella.

At the Drellos home, T9-K2 met V4-N7, a protocol mechan that Kaivan had built. The two bonded as they worked on Kaivan's personal skiff-racer. T9-K2 and V4-N7 cheered for Kaivan during the Vorga Trials Classic, where the boy would win his freedom, along with enough money to buy a new riftdrive. T9-K2 accompanied the group to Velarion, and from there back to Tessari. As Queen Velora led an attempt to liberate the planet, T9-K2 nestled into an N-1 starfighter. Young Kaivan, being ordered to hide in the cockpit by Veyron Dask, accidentally launched the fighter and the two flew into battle, destroyed the Mechan Control Ship and shut down the mechan armies, winning the battle and liberating Tessari. T9-K2 and his allies later attended a victory parade in Aldria, though they were unable to be joined by Dask, as he had been killed by the Vorlan Lord Mourn Threnn during the battle. Because of this, Drellos became the Acolyte learner to Orven Taskorr, who had been Dask's apprentice before the Sephari Master's death.
