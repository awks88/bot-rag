# Vorlan

The Vorlan, also referred to as the Vorlan Order, was an ancient religious order of Synth Flux-wielders devoted to the Umbra of the Synth Flux. Driven by their raw emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Vorlan were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under Mourn Sarnex, the Dark Lord of the Vorlan who achieved his order's goal of galactic conquest after a millennium of plotting. Within a generation, however, the deaths of Mourn Sarnex and Mourn Velgor marked the end of the Vorlan Lords. Though the Vorlan ultimately went extinct with Sarnex's demise, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient temples that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt.

Thousands of years before the Cinder Wars, the Sephari Order was fractured by a schism that began when a rogue Sephari and his followers turned away from the Lumen of the Synth Flux, believing the Umbra was the path to true power. Having relocated to the planet Vaulor in the Far Verge Territories, their movement ultimately evolved into the Vorlan Order, resulting in a war between the former brothers and sisters in the Synth Flux as the Sephari Knights rallied to the defense of the Old Concord. Although the Vorlan were driven off of the Core World Velarion by 5000 BVR, centuries of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the Old Concord's collapse. Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the last war against the Sephari, the Vorlan were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; Mourn Vorrane survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, Mourn Zannah, before being entombed on Vaulor, which became known as Vaulmir. Henceforth, the Vorlan was governed by the Rule of Two, a new philosophy dictating that only two Vorlan Lords—a Vorlan Master and a Vorlan apprentice—could exist at any given time. As a result, the Vorlan were all but forgotten while the Sephari focused their efforts on preserving the new age of peace under the modern Astral Concord.

The Vorlan remained in hiding for a millennium until the rise of Mourn Sarnex and Mourn Threnn in 32 BVR. Sarnex, trained by Mourn Veshk, hid in plain sight as Delegate Velran Castigon of Tessari. Orchestrating the blockade and invasion of his homeworld by the Commerce League, Sarnex maneuvered the Astral Conclave into electing him to the office of Supreme Praetor. His entire chancellery would encompass the final years of the Concord. Over the next decade Sarnex sowed the seeds of discontent within the galactic populace with the help of his new apprentice, the former Sephari Master Count Maren, who adopted the name of Mourn Sevrin and became the public face of the Independent Movement for Self-Determination. The Severite Crisis culminated in the formation of the League of Severed Worlds and the creation of the Severite Mechan Army which, in turn, compelled the Conclave to support the establishment of the Grand Army of the Concord. Bimo Trask, acting in place of Delegate Velora, started a motion to grant the Supreme Praetor emergency powers.

The Cinder Wars began with the First Battle of Karnak in 22 BVR, after which entire armies of battle mechans and vatborn troopers deployed across the galaxy, making war and devastation commonplace for the first time since the formation of the Concord. In the final months of the Cinder Wars, Sarnex sacrificed and betrayed Sevrin as part of his plot to turn Kaivan Drellos, the Sephari Order's prophesied Fated One, to the Umbra. Playing on Drellos's fear of losing his secret wife, Delegate Sennra Velora, Castigon revealed his deception and true identity, while claiming that the Umbra was the path to immortality. Members of the Sephari High Council, having learned from Drellos's report that the praetor was, in fact, a Vorlan Lord, attempted to arrest Castigon, but the praetor resisted. Torn between his faith in the Sephari and the fate he foresaw for his wife, Drellos ultimately chose the Vorlan by betraying Dovan Krell, allowing Sarnex to kill the Sephari Order's great champion.

Using the incident in the praetor's office to frame the Sephari Order for treason, Sarnex declared Directive Null was in effect, marking all Sephari as enemies of the state and susceptible to execution. The vatborn troopers' programming turned them against their Sephari Generals, who were betrayed and executed across the galaxy. With the majority of the order falling victim to the Great Sephari Purge, Sarnex announced the creation of a new regime, transforming the Concord into the First Astral Dominion in 19 BVR. With the former Kaivan Drellos, renamed Mourn Velgor, at his side, the self-proclaimed Astral Overlord's rise to power was complete, and the Vorlan were restored to dominance after centuries of plotting.
