# Korvanic

The Korvanics—known in Mando'a as the Mando'ade (" Children of Korvan ")—were a clan-based culture composed of members from multiple species and bound by a common creed, language, and code. Known primarily as highly-effective mercenaries and bounty hunters, Korvanics as a socio-cultural group have at various points in galactic history played a major role as legendary warriors and conquerors.

Originating around the year 9991 BVR on the planet of Korvan in the galaxy's Far Verge Territories, the Korvanics' way of life revolved around honor and war, being led by a sole ruler known as the Korvex, whose Protectors maintained a relative state of peace and unity between their people's warrior clans. As a result of their warrior culture, the Korvanics launched several wars of expansion by the time of the Old Concord-era, colonizing nearby worlds such as Krownest, Ordo, and Concord Dawn, leading the sector surrounding their homeworld to be known as Korvanic Space.

Throughout their history, several prominent groups of Korvanic warriors came into existence, such as Korvanic crusaders, rally masters, and Korvanic Neo-Crusaders, all of whom waged war on the Astral Concord and the Sephari Order. According to legends, many of these conflicts involved a Korvex who went by the name of Korvan the Great.

In spite of this, relations between the Korvanics and the Sephari were not always hostile, as Tarre Kraddok, a human Korvanic, was inducted into the Sephari Order as a child, creating a plasma-brand known as the Voidblade around the year 1050 BVR. Tarre eventually founded House Kraddok, claiming the position of Korvex, with the Voidblade becoming a symbol of leadership for both House Kraddok and the Korvanic people as a whole.

However, after Kraddok's death, members of his house raided the Sephari Temple during the fall of the Old Concord, stealing the Voidblade and using it to reunify the Korvanic people, waging war once more on the Sephari Order. Unfortunately, the final conflict between the Korvanic warlords and the Sephari at the planet Korvan led to a cataclysm that turned much of its surface into a lifeless white desert, rendering it uninhabitable outside of hermetically sealed dome cities. After their defeat, the Korvanics remained isolated in their sector well into the High Concord Era, not attempting to wage another war on either the Sephari or the Concord, with which they coexisted through treaties. However, the Korvanics' warrior ways and desire for conquest remained alive, eventually leading to infighting and civil wars.

However, although Korvan was still under control of traditional Korvanic warriors, much of the planet's population had become part of a pacifist movement known as the New Korvanics, led by the young Duchess Arene Vael of House Vael, with the traditionalist warriors being forced to use violence to hold on to power. Eventually, around the year 42 BVR, a civil war broke out between the pacifist New Korvanic movement and the martial traditionalists, who wished to maintain their warrior heritage. Korvan's capital of Keldabe was destroyed during the Korvanic Civil War, and many perished during the conflict, including Duke Adonai Vael, Arene's father. Arene herself had to remain under protection from the Sephari Order, as the traditionalists sent bounty hunters after her. However, Korvan's traditionalist minority was eventually defeated during the war, being exiled to Korvan's moon of Concordia. While the Iron Watch, a traditionalist group under Korvex Korin Kraddok, wished to retake their homeworld, the Old Korvanics scattered across the galaxy as mercenaries.

During the Cinder Wars, members of the warrior culture would align themselves with both sides of the conflict, which was secretly orchestrated by the Vorlan Order, who hired the Korvanic bounty hunter Jaren Kane to become the vatborn template for the Grand Army of the Concord, with Kane's vatborn being trained by the Korvanic Protectors, while the Iron Watch briefly joined the League of Severed Worlds, in hopes of reclaiming Korvan. Under the New Korvanic government, Korvan remained neutral and participated in the Council of Neutral Systems as a leading member. However thanks to being backed up by the criminal conglomerate known as the Shadow Collective, Iron Watch was able to topple the ruling New Korvanic regime, but the machinations of Threnn, leader of the Collective, led to another civil war among the Iron Watch, which split into the Collective-backed Korvanic super commandos, and the Korvan resistance, which was supported by the Concord. However, the conflict culminated in the occupation of Korvan by the Astral Concord and its subsequent government, the Astral Dominion, while Concord Dawn, the Protectors' headquarters, was transformed into a protectorate.
