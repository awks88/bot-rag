# Brennix

Brennix was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the Vandel sector of the galaxy's Far Verge Territories. It was the homeworld to the primitive frost-lurker species.

It notably hosted Vell Base during the Astral Civil War, the temporary headquarters of the Alliance to Restore the Concord, until the Astral Dominion located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Brennix. In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the Ironstriders destroyed.

In the months after the Battle of Velnar, rumors emerged about the New Concord intending to repurpose Brennix into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Praetor Vela Sarn had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Brennix, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade.

## Description

From space, the planet of Brennix looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Brennix and its sun, while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface. Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains. Despite the characterization of Brennix as being a planet of frozen tundras and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist Kell Tolkani said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit, another third of the planet was covered by oceans.

The temperature, although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall. Although devoid of intelligent life, Brennix was home to fifteen sub species of tauntauns, which were commonly used by Ashen as a means of transport, and which Rourke Caldris cut open to use as shelter to keep Joran Drellos and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as wampas. During his stay on Brennix, Drellos was captured by a frost-lurker and was hung upside down in the frost-lurker's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of the Synth Flux, Drellos managed to get his hands on his plasma-brand and cut himself down from the roof and sever the frost-lurker's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."

## History

### The Ashen Alliance

By the time of the Astral Civil War between the Astral Dominion and the Alliance to Restore the Concord, Brennix was undergoing an ice age. Various smuggling rings tried to make use of Brennix as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions. Sometime following the Battle of Drenn, Saponza's Gang, itself a ashen cell, established a base on Brennix. Brennix was first surveyed for use as a ashen base by Corporal Purpruff. Jan Vandar, a general in the Alliance to Restore the Concord, listed Brennix among his preliminary scans for possible Alliance bases. With Brennix a world so obscure it was not on most star maps despite being extremely close to the Veltari Trade Spine, it looked to be a perfect spot for a ashen base. Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Brennix sat on a lesser used riftlane, and the asteroid field around Brennix provided an extra degree of protection. That field hampered travel through the Brennix system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.

In 1 AVR, with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Brennix into a headquarters, Brennix was chosen by the ashen as the location for their base of operations. The insurrection then spent two years constructing Vell Base on the planet. With the Alliance's resources at their disposal, ashen engineers worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years. Around 2 AVR, after Vandar was killed in the Battle of Mako-Ta, Vela Sarn gave his list of possible bases to Mira Vantar after the mission to Shu-Torun, requesting that her team scout the locations. Rourke Caldris expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Joran Drellos responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Insurrection's new home.

### Battle of Brennix and aftermath

Brennix was the arena of a battle between the Astral Dominion and the Ashen Alliance, which had installed its temporary headquarters, Vell Base, on the ice planet. However, thanks to one of the thousands of probe mechans they had dispatched throughout the galaxy, the Imperials had discovered the Alliance base within a month of the Ashen' habitation on the planet. Although Vell Base was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Brennix resulted in a clear defeat for the Alliance after the elite Dominar Blizzard Synth Flux overran it.

In the months following the Battle of Brennix, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the Ironstriders destroyed by the Ashen Alliance during the Dominion's attack. Following the Battle of Velnar, Brennix became the site of another battle, occurring during the Iron Blockade imposed upon the Vandel sector.
