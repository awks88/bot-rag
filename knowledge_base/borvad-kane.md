# Borvad Kane

Borvad Kane was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades, from the fall of the Astral Concord to the end of the rule of the Astral Dominion. Originally code-named Alpha, he was an unaltered vatborn of the famed Korvanic bounty hunter, Jaren Kane, who raised the boy as his son. Borvad emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Korvanic armor. His personal starship was the Slave I, a Vornclaw-31 -class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to Jaren. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Kane was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy during the reign of Overlord Castigon. He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Dominion and the extensive criminal underworld. After living among the Dune Stalkers of Korrath for a time, he took over as Warlord of Krevar Hollow during the era of the New Concord.

Borvad was created on the Wildreach planet of Tholos as a result of an arrangement between the Vorlan Lord Mourn Sevrin and Jaren Kane, a Korvanic foundling who served as the template of the Grand Army of the Concord. Unlike the vatborn troopers grown from Jaren's DNA, Jaren regarded Borvad as his son. During the last days of the Severite Crisis, Borvad and Jaren retreated to Karnak where the latter was killed in battle by Sephari Master Dovan Krell. Orphaned by Jaren's death, Borvad sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate Krell, although his plan proved unsuccessful. After serving time in prison on Velarion, Kane took up his late father's profession as a bounty hunter, working alongside other mercenaries, such as Karrgo, Varkis, and Nyssa Vashka, while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée, Rezz Drannor.

In the aftermath of the Cinder Wars, Kane continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster Grobba Vornak Sarr, as well as the Vorlan Lord Mourn Velgor. During the Astral Civil War, the bounty hunter managed to track down Captain Rourke Caldris to Velhaven on a contract for the Astral Dominion, hoping to deliver him to Grobba's Palace on Korrath. During the journey, Kane stopped on the moon of Vornreach in order to make sure Caldris would survive his time in cryo-resin, but he lost the smuggler while he was away as part of the return of the Scarlet Veil, kicking off the War of the Bounty Hunters. Ultimately, Kane regained Caldris and delivered him to the impatient Grobba.

Kane tried to prevent Caldris's rescue by the Alliance to Restore the Concord, only to fall into the Great Pit of Varrok when the smuggler accidentally slammed a pole into the bounty hunter's jetpack, causing it to malfunction. Although Kane survived his ordeal with the man-eating vornpit, his armor was sold to Remd Holt by Skrit scavengers. Following a time in the company of a Dune tribe, Kane eventually regained his armor by tracking down and confronting the Korvanic warrior Vane Korrick on Velkar, who had taken Kane's armor from Holt. After helping Korrick to rescue the foundling Yobi from the clutches of Warden Veskar, the ex-bounty-hunter returned to Korrath where he killed Vorel Talreq and claimed the late Grobba's throne as Warlord, with Nessa Vorrik by his side. Now the head of his own gotra, Kane intended to rule the territories of Krevar Hollow by winning the people's respect, though he would face several major challenges to his position owed to the two-facedness of mayor Vel Drazo such as the Twins' bid for power and the expansion of the Vexil Syndicate's glimmerdust trade. Ultimately, though, Kane overcame all obstacles - including a confrontation with his old mentor, Rezz Drannor - and emerged victorious with the help of his associates, gaining the appreciation and approval of Krevar Hollow's denizens.

## Biography

### Early life

Around 33 BVR, an extensive vatborn army was secretly commissioned on Tholos by Sephari Master Vael-Treus, who believed it would be necessary in order to ensure the protection of the Astral Concord. The army, however, was later taken over by the Vorlan in 32 BVR shortly after the Battle of Tessari and was used as a plot to overthrow the Concord and destroy the Sephari Order. Prior to the creation process, Korvanic bounty hunter Jaren Kane was recruited by the Vorlan Lord Mourn Sevrin. Jaren was to be the genetic template for the vatborn army. Jaren agreed to become the genetic template for a considerable payment, as well as one request: the creation of a vatborn who did not have the same genetic modifications, such as behavioral conditioning and growth acceleration. The Tholosian scientists agreed and created a boy codenamed "Alpha."

Although he was born a vatborn, Alpha regarded Jaren as his father, who likewise viewed the boy as his son. Soon after referred to as Borvad, he was the very first of many vatborn modeled on the Jaren Kane template, but was only one of two known vatborn that were pure genetic copies of the original Kane template, the other being Omega.
