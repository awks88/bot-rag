# Sennra Velora Tarrin

Sennra Velora Tarrin, also simply known as Sennra Velora, was a human female politician who represented the people of Tessari during the final years of the Astral Concord. Prior to her career as a delegate in the Astral Conclave, Velora was the elected ruler of the Royal House of Tessari. A political idealist, she advocated for the preservation of democracy as well as a peaceful resolution to the Cinder Wars. However, her secret marriage to the Sephari Knight Kaivan Drellos would have a lasting effect on the future of the galaxy for decades to come.

Born in 46 BVR during the Age of the Concord, Sennra Tarrin was raised by her parents, Varro Tarrin and his wife Yenna, on the peaceful Mid Verge world of Tessari. Despite the humble background of the Tarrin family, Sennra was identified early for her potential in public service. Devoting her life to civic duty, she was elected queen and, therefore, adopted the regnal name "Velora" in 32 BVR. Due to the machinations of her advisor, Delegate Velran Castigon, Velora's reign was put to the test by the Commerce League's blockade and subsequent invasion of Tessari. Unable to fight the Combine's army of battle mechans without assistance, the queen beseeched the Astral Conclave on Velarion for help, only to see her efforts hampered by bureaucracy and procedure. Although she ultimately liberated her homeworld with the help of the Sephari Order and the Naari Grand Army, her decision to cast a Vote of No Confidence against Halvor Calvex enabled Castigon to become the Supreme Praetor.

Following her reign, Velora joined the Conclave and struggled to resolve the Severite Crisis through diplomacy, becoming a leading voice in the Loyalist Committee by 22 BVR. After surviving an assassination attempt planned by the Commerce League, she was removed from the capital and sent back to Tessari under the protection of Acolyte Kaivan Drellos. Though the Sephari Code forbade personal relationships, deeming attachment, and possession unbecoming of a Sephari, the two gradually became closer as their feelings for each other grew. In the aftermath of the First Battle of Karnak, Velora secretly married her Sephari protector, despite her own reservations about their relationship and the consequences they would face if the truth ever came to light. Their subsequent interactions were limited throughout the Cinder Wars, with Velora continuing to advocate peaceful solutions in the Conclave while Drellos served on the front lines as a Sephari General in the Grand Army of the Concord.

In the waning months of the pan-galactic conflict, Velora revealed to her husband that she was pregnant. As a result of Drellos's fear of loss, he was seduced by the Umbra of the Synth Flux, believing that by becoming a Vorlan Lord he would gain the power to prevent his wife from dying in childbirth. Velora witnessed Overlord Castigon's declaration that her fears had come true; the Concord had become the Astral Dominion. After she learned of her husband's transformation into a Vorlan, she confronted him on Cindara in the hopes that he had not truly changed, but he instead attempted to kill her. She ultimately died of a broken heart after giving birth to the twins Joran Drellos and Mira Velora Drellos. With her dying breath, she insisted that there was still good in the former Kaivan Drellos, who embraced the persona of Mourn Velgor throughout the reign of the Dominion.

Velora's children played a major role in the fragmentation of the Dominion and the redemption of her husband. Like her mother, Mira entered public service and became a flag-bearer for the democratic ideals of liberty and justice as a leader in the Alliance to Restore the Concord and its successor, the New Concord, before becoming the founding general of the Defiance against the Crimson Pact—a successor state to the Astral Dominion. Joran, on the other hand, was trained in the ways of the Sephari like his father before him and was a celebrated war hero of the Ashen Alliance. Through Joran's efforts to redeem his father back to the light, Velora's last words proved true when Kaivan killed the Overlord during the Battle of Velnar.

## Biography

### Early life

A human female of the Tarrin family, the young woman best remembered as Sennra Velora was born Sennra Tarrin on the bountiful planet of Tessari in 46 BVR. She was the younger daughter of Varro and Yenna Tarrin and the sister of Tanys Tarrin. Her father was a friend of Delegate Onaconda Farr, who represented the solar system of Rodia in the Conclave of the Astral Concord. Tarrin was very fond of the Rodian, whom she referred to as "Uncle Ono."

As a child, she baked every day and traveled with her father on relief missions to offer aid to suffering planets. When Tarrin was seven, she traveled with her father and Farr to Shadda-Bi-Boran to help relocate the population in the wake of an ecological disaster. Unfortunately, the effort resulted in failure, as the Shadda-Bi-Boran could not adapt to their new homeworld. It led her to declare later in life that she would try 10,000 times in order to save one person.
