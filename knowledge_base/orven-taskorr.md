# Orven Taskorr

Orven Taskorr was a legendary Synth Flux-sensitive human male Sephari Master who served on the Sephari High Council during the final years of the Concord Era. As a High Sephari General, Taskorr served in the Grand Army of the Concord that fought against the Severite Mechan Army during the Cinder Wars, and was nicknamed " the Negotiator." Taskorr, however, was forced into exile as a result of the Great Sephari Purge initiated by the Concord's own vatborn troopers when Concord Supreme Praetor, secretly Mourn Sarnex, implemented Directive Null. As a mentor, Taskorr was responsible for training two members of the Drellos family, Kaivan and Joran Drellos, both of whom served in turn as his Acolyte in the ways of the Synth Flux.

Taskorr was born on the planet Velhar in 57 BVR. He was Synth Flux-sensitive, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into the Sephari Order as a child. Raised in the Sephari Grand Temple on the planet Velarion, Taskorr was assigned to Sephari Master Veyron Dask. In 32 BVR, Taskorr attained the rank of Sephari Knight by defeating the Vorlan Lord Mourn Threnn during the Invasion of Tessari. He also chose to train Kaivan Drellos—the prophesied Fated One who was destined to destroy the Vorlan and restore balance to the Synth Flux—having promised Dask who died in the plasma-brand duel against Threnn.

With the advent of the Cinder Wars in 22 BVR, Taskorr adopted the military rank of High Sephari General in the Grand Army of the Astral Concord, and was given command of the Open Circle Fleet, the Third Systems Army, and the 7th Sky Corps. Vatborn Marshal Commander Bren served as his second-in-command throughout the galaxy-wide conflict, with their forces achieving multiple victories over the League of Severed Worlds. In the midst of the war, his old enemy Threnn re-emerged into galactic affairs, bent on revenge against Taskorr for his debilitating defeat decades prior. Though they clashed in heated duels on multiple occasions, Threnn failed to end Taskorr's life or cripple his spirit, despite murdering his former love, Arene Vael, Duchess of Korvan.

In 19 BVR, Taskorr killed the Severite Supreme Martial Commander, General Skarl, during the Battle of Velkoth, effectively ending the Cinder Wars. He later survived the initial onslaught of the Great Sephari Purge against the Sephari Order, which resulted from the machinations of the Vorlan Lord Mourn Sarnex. After discovering that Drellos had turned to the Umbra of the Synth Flux, becoming Sarnex' Vorlan apprentice under the name of Mourn Velgor, Taskorr defeated his former Acolyte on Cindara and exiled himself to the Far Verge planet Korrath as the Astral Dominion rose to power. Adopting a hermit lifestyle as " Ben Taskorr," he watched over Velgor's and Delegate Sennra Velora's son, Joran.

Ten years later, Taskorr left exile to rescue Mira Vantar, Joran's twin sister, and was pursued by the Dominion's Tribunal and Velgor himself. Later, Taskorr was hunted down once again by Threnn, whom he finally slew to protect the young Joran. Taskorr began the boy's Sephari training in 1 BVR, and soon after encountered Velgor aboard the first Void Core, where he sacrificed himself to ensure that Joran and his allies escaped from the Vorlan Lord. In death, Taskorr became one with the Synth Flux which allowed him to continue guiding Joran throughout the Astral Civil War. In 4 AVR, Taskorr reunited with his former student, Kaivan Drellos, who died destroying his Vorlan Master in order to save his son. Taskorr was remembered as a legendary Sephari Master during the rise of the New Concord; Ben Caldris, the son of Joran's twin sister, Princess Mira Vantar, was named after Taskorr. Caldris's turn to the Umbra left Joran disillusioned with the Sephari Order's legacy, and he held Taskorr responsible for Kaivan's transformation into Velgor. However, Joran's faith would eventually be restored with the aid of Mourn Sarnex's granddaughter Sefa, to whom Taskorr would lend his voice and strength in a final battle and victory against her resurrected ancestor on Vauldor in 35 AVR.

## Biography

### Early life

Orven Taskorr was born on the planet Velhar in 57 BVR. Despite being very young when he left his family, he still had glimpses of his father's hands and his mother's shawl, and he remembered a baby he believed to be his brother although he barely remembered him.

### Joining the Sephari Order

When Taskorr was discovered to be Synth Flux-sensitive, he was taken from his homeworld to the Sephari Temple on the Core Systems planet Velarion to be trained as a Sephari within the first six months of his life. In the Sephari crèche he was placed with other Sephari novices in the Kybuck Clan during his early years, including his friends Gehren and Prie. Taskorr received basic Initiate training alongside his peers in the Sephari Order's Temple and began training in several simple Synth Flux techniques by the age of three in 54 BVR.
