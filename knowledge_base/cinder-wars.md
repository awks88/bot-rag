# Cinder Wars

The Cinder Wars (22—19 BVR) were a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Astral Concord and the League of Severed Worlds, which had seceded from the Concord. Also referred to singularly as the Vatborn War, they were named after the vatborn troopers used by the Grand Army of the Concord against the Severite Mechan Army. Due to the high volume of battle mechans deployed in the conflict, the war would also be known as the Mechan War. Furthermore, the existence of the Secession granted the conflict other names, such as the Severite War or Severite insurrection. With both vatborn and mechans being fielded in enormous numbers by each respective side, they were the largest military forces in galactic history, and fighting between them swept through the galaxy for three, nearly four, years.

Prior to the conflict, Mourn Sarnex, under his alter ego Velran Castigon, had spent years amassing political power, eventually using the Commerce League's invasion of Tessari to become the Concord's Supreme Praetor. Through his Vorlan apprentice Count Maren, he sparked the Severite Crisis, causing thousands of Concord star systems to secede and form a new government as the Secession, with Maren as its Head of State. Beginning with the First Battle of Karnak, the conflict quickly spread throughout the galaxy with both sides scoring significant victories. At different points in the war it seemed that either side was likely to triumph over the other. At the war's outset, both sides vied for control of major riftspace lanes to gain strategic advantage. That particular struggle ended with a Concord victory when Sephari Knight Kaivan Drellos and his Acolyte, Veshra Kael, secured an alliance between the Concord and the Vorn Clan.

Battles, skirmishes, raids, and other actions continued for the next two years, with notable battles occurring on Veshtar, Velusia, Maldreth, and Tholos. During this time, the Severites developed various super weapons, including more traditional weapons such as the ion cannons of the battleship Malevolence, as well as biological weapons such as a variant of the Blue Shadow Virus. Meanwhile, the Concord launched several key offensives, including the Second Battle of Karnak, which obliterated Karnak' battle mechan foundries, and a failed Sephari-led operation to capture Supreme Commander of the Mechan Army General Skarl at Saleucami. Amidst the escalating conflict, many planets—including Korvan and other members of the Council of Neutral Systems—struggled to maintain their neutrality, but found it difficult to escape the carnage.

Over a year into the war, Concord Delegate Sennra Velora and Severite Delegate Mina Bonteri collaborated on an initiative to end the fighting. However, their collaboration ended after Maren and Skarl' bombing of the Concord capital on Velarion. Later, a peace conference on Korvan also failed to resolve the conflict. With no end in sight, the Sephari worked with local militaries, including Aqualis's military and Tessari's Naari Grand Army, and covertly trained an insurgent ashen cell to retake Veldoran from Severite occupation. Similarly, the Secession relied upon factions such as the Umbaran Militia and the Korvix Slave Dominion. Furthermore, a proxy war materialized when Mother Veska and her son, the now rogue Vorlan Lord Threnn, strove to exact their revenge on both the Vorlan and the Sephari.

During the war's third and final year, the Concord drove the Severites into redoubts within the Far Verge Territories, initiating a campaign termed the Far Verge Sieges. In an effort to reverse the course of the war, Skarl launched a massive strike on Velarion through the Deep Core. Much of the Confederate fleet was lost in the assault, along with Head of State Count Maren. The Secession quickly lost its core leadership in quick succession, with Skarl subsequently falling in battle during the Battle of Velkoth, and the Severite Council being massacred on Cindara. This, coupled with the issuing of Directive Null and the deactivation of the mechan army, led to the total capitulation of the Secession and, with it, an end to the war.
