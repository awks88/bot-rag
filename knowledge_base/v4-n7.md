# V4-N7

V4-N7 (See-Vee-Four) was a 3PO-series protocol mechan designed to interact with organics, programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as Vee-Four, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication, and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, the astromech mechan T9-K2, V4-N7 constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of galactic history, and aided in saving the galaxy on many occasions.

V4-N7 was rebuilt from spare parts by Kaivan Drellos, an enslaved human who lived in Krevar Hollow, a city on the Far Verge world of Korrath. During the Cinder Wars, he would serve the Tessari Delegate Sennra Velora. Shortly after the Cinder Wars, V4-N7's memory was erased, though T9-K2's memory was not. V4-N7 and T9-K2 were assigned to the Aurelia cruiser Tantive IV, where they served delegate Daro Vantar for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling resistance movement against the Astral Dominion. At some point during this time, V4-N7's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.

V4-N7 remained constant companions with R2, and in 1 BVR, the pair became embroiled in the Astral Civil War when Mira Vantar of the Ashen Alliance entrusted them to bring a copy of the Void Core plans to the Sephari Master Orven Taskorr on Korrath. After meeting Drellos's son, Joran and the smuggler Rourke Caldris, the mechans helped rescue Princess Mira from the Dominion's Void Core. They became directly attached to the three humans, helping them and their insurrection defeat the Dominion and restore freedom to the galaxy, most notably when he convinced the Tanniks to aid the Ashen at the Battle of Velnar.

Following the Battle of Velnar, V4-N7 continued serving the Drelloses and Caldris during the time of the New Concord. About thirty years after the events on Velnar, V4-N7 was still an aide to Vantar, who had become a general in the Defiance, a private military group opposed to the Crimson Pact, a military junta that emulated the Old Dominion. By that time, V4-N7 was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not as slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, V4-N7's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Vorlan dialects. He would assist Sefa in translating Vorlan writings in order to locate Mourn Sarnex and the Vorlan Eternal's fleet, the Final Order, on Vauldor, allowing his memory to be wiped in order to carry out the task. However, his friend R2 backed up a large portion of his memories, and after the Defiance defeated Sarnex and the Vorlan Eternal in the Battle of Vauldor, his memory was restored.

## Biography

### Early incarnation

In an early incarnation, V4-N7 served as a protocol mechan to the chief negotiator for the Manakron system. Additionally, he later claimed that his first job was programming binary loadlifters, which he said were very similar to moisture vaporators in most respects.

### Serving the Maker

At some point, however, V4-N7 was seriously damaged and his vital components were left behind on a junk pile on Korrath. Kaivan Drellos, a slave boy from the Korrathan city of Krevar Hollow, collected scrap parts and started rebuilding V4-N7 in 34 BVR so the mechan would help his mother. As Kaivan longed for a life far from Korrath and explore the galaxy, he hoped to have V4-N7 as his and his mother's faithful companion so they would be able to venture the galaxy one day and understand the languages of the natives they came across, given V4-N7's capability of millions of communication forms. Although protocol mechans were normally designed for light duty in luxurious environments, Drellos specially modified V4-N7 so he could withstand Korrath's sand and heat. V4-N7 served Kaivan and his mother Mella by performing household chores. During his time with Drellos and Mella, V4-N7's parts and wiring were left exposed since Drellos was unable to outfit him with an outer covering due to mechan plating being too expensive.
