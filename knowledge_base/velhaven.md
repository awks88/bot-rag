# Velhaven

Velhaven was a gas giant in the star system of the same name in the Vandel sector, its riches manifested in the form of rare tibanna gas. Attracting mining interests and remaining unaffiliated in galactic affairs, Ugnaughts were employed to extract tibanna from repulsorlifted facilities whilst Velhaven's mostly human population resided in the open upper levels, exposed to a thin layer of breathable atmosphere in an area known as a " Life Zone."

## Description

Velhaven was an immense gas giant located in the vicinity of the Vandel sector, a desolate sector of the galaxy. Velhaven had two moons, and its gaseous mass contained a thin stratum of habitable atmosphere above a source of rare and costly tibanna gas, although there was no habitable land or water on the planet. The planet completed a full orbit around its star every 14 standard-years and a full rotation every 12 standard-hours. Viewed from the upper levels of Mist Spire, in the breathable layer of the world, sunsets lasted 2 hours.

Tibanna gas was harvested and refined in several mining complexes, including Mist Spire, an opulent metropolis suspended in the planet's huge billowing clouds, and Chinook Station, a fuel depot and refueling station. The Ugnaught city of Ugnorgrad also was located over the planet, being ruled by King Ozz in the time after the Battle of Velnar. Mist Spire processed tibanna to be sold off-world.

Velhaven was home to many types of life, which was rare for a gas giant. The planet had two sister worlds, from which it was separated by an asteroid belt called Velser's Ring.

Swimming pools were considered a rare luxury on Velhaven.

## History

During the Cinder Wars, General Skarl led Severite forces and invaded several planets and blockaded several vital riftspace routes leading to Velhaven, Drungar, and Aqualis within the space of a single month. During the Astral Civil War, Velhaven remained unaffiliated, but that did not prevent the Astral Dominion from garrisoning the planet and seizing Mist Spire. However, with the Dominion's defeat at Velnar, the gas giant and its prosperous mining colony ended up recovering their freedom.

Following the Battle of Vauldor in 35 AVR, Velhaven was in open insurrection against the Crimson Pact.
