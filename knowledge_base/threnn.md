# Threnn

Threnn was a Synth Flux-sensitive Vaelmorni Korthul male who was apprenticed to the Vorlan Lord Mourn Sarnex, and after his defeat by the Sephari Orven Taskorr, he established himself as a crime lord during the Cinder Wars and the Dominar Era. The renegade Vorlan Lord was a formidable warrior, driven by hatred against the Sephari and his former Vorlan Master.

Born in 54 BVR during the time of the Astral Concord, Threnn was a son of Mother Veska, who led the Duskwraiths of Vaelmorn. Taken as a child by Mourn Sarnex, Mourn Threnn was molded into a deadly Dark Lord of the Vorlan, growing in hatred of the Sephari Knights who served the Concord and the Lumen of the Synth Flux—while the Sephari Order had deemed the Vorlan extinct for a thousand years, Threnn's power-hungry master set his Grand Plan into motion with the Commerce League's Invasion of Tessari in 32 BVR. Mourn Threnn announced the return of the Vorlan on Korrath, where he tried to kill the Sephari emissaries of the Concord's Supreme Praetor. However, Threnn's failure to do so allowed Sephari Master Veyron Dask and his pupil, Acolyte Orven Taskorr, to bring Queen Velora of Tessari and a boy strong in the Synth Flux—exceptionally so, to the extent that Dask believed this "Kaivan Drellos" to be the prophesied Fated One who would bring balance to the galaxy—to Velarion, the heart of the Concord.

Nevertheless, Mourn Sarnex used the resultant crisis in the Conclave to elevate his public persona, Delegate Castigon of Tessari, to the position of Supreme Praetor. The Queen and Sephari, meanwhile, joined forces with the native Naari on Tessari to throw off the yoke of the Commerce League. Mourn Threnn, having been dispatched by his master to oversee the Combine leadership, was redirected to handle the Sephari. Threnn dealt a fatal blow to Veyron Dask, but Taskorr in turn defeated Threnn by bisecting him at the waist, with the Dark Lord falling to the abyss and presumed dead. He was actually alive, sustained by his rage in the wastelands of Lotho Minor. Around the same time, Sarnex took on Dask's Sephari Master, Maren, as his new apprentice. A decade afterward, Maren set the second part of the Vorlan plan into motion with the start of the Cinder Wars.

As the galaxy plunged into war, Mother Veska plotted against Maren and had one of her sons, Vrenn Garokk, seek out Threnn. Indeed, the defeated Korthul was brought back to Vaelmorn and had his sanity and mobility restored by Veska's magick. Threnn trained his newfound brother in the ways of the Vorlan, initially wanting to take revenge against Orven Taskorr. After the brothers were defeated by the Sephari, they took their chance to increase their powers with a brazen takeover of Korvan, the erstwhile neutral world torn between the interests of the Concord and the Severites. Threnn seized control of the self-styled Iron Watch and used them terrorise Korvan while he expanded his criminal enterprise, known as the Shadow Collective—the Vexil Syndicate, Umbral Cartel, and the Vorn Clan were coerced to join forces under the underworld's new crime lord—until he caught the attention of Sarnex, who killed Threnn's brother and captured Threnn.

Threnn was rescued by his Korvanic loyalists and immediately tried to reconsolidate his Shadow Collective. However, he and his mother's desire for revenge against Mourn Sarnex was checked by the Dark Lord, who instead decimated their homeworld of Vaelmorn with the use of the Severites led by Maren and General Skarl. Mother Veska herself was killed while protecting Threnn, and his syndicates were weakened. While he regathered with his loyalists on Korvan, Threnn sensed that his former master's Grand Plan was coming to its climax and wished to lure Kaivan Drellos—the Sephari Knight trained by Orven Taskorr whom he correctly believed was central to Sarnex' schemes—alongside Taskorr himself to Korvan. Threnn's thirst for revenge was foiled again when Concord forces intervening on the side of the Korvan resistance were instead led by Drellos's old apprentice, Veshra Kael.

While Threnn and Kael fought at the Siege of Korvan, Sarnex successfully executed his plan to destroy the Sephari Order and anoint himself Astral Overlord. With the support of some Korvanic and Korthul loyalists, Threnn continued trying to control the underworld but operated more clandestinely, away from the prying apparatus of the new Dominar regime. He was known in criminal circles as the Shadow Lord and was pursued by the Overlord's Arbiters, who simply called him the Shadow. In the Dominion's later years, Threnn secretly coordinated the Five Crime Syndicates and controlled the Scarlet Veil syndicate through Varek Salin and Vessa.
