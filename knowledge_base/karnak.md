# Karnak

Karnak, referred to as Karnessa by some natives, was the desert home planet of the Karnaki. It hosted the League of Severed Worlds major battle mechan foundries and was the site of the First Battle of Karnak, the opening conflict of the Cinder Wars, as well as the subsequent invasion by the Astral Concord. During and after the war, the Void Core was constructed above the planet. Five years after the conflict's conclusion and transformation of the Astral Concord, the Astral Dominion would garrison the planet heavily and attempt to mine the planet's asteroids for minerals. At least five years after the Cinder Wars, the Dominion moved the Void Core to another construction site and the planet was sterilized by the Dominion as a security measure, an act which resulted in the virtual elimination of the planet's native population.

## Description

Situated in the Karnak system of the Arkanis sector in the Far Verge Territories, the planet of Karnak was 43,000 light years from the Astral Core, and less than a parsec away from the neighboring Far Verge planet of Korrath. It was the homeworld of the Karnaki, a species of sentient insectoids. It had a breathable atmosphere, but a hot and arid climate. Its irradiated surface was covered in harsh, rocky deserts, marked by mesas and buttes. Both its rocks and sky were tinted in shades of red. Surface water was scarce, amounting to only 5% of the entire total planetary surface. However, Karnak had frequent flash floods. From space, it could be seen that Karnak had awe-inspiring rocky rings. The planet was orbited by fifteen moons, four of which were major and included the icy moon of Bahryn.

## History

### Ancient history

At some point in the ancient history of Karnak, a comet crashed in one of the planet's many moons. The collision debris not only formed the famous rings of Karnak, but also formed a field of small asteroids which would sometimes rain down on the planet's surface.

### Under the Concord

Astral colonizers were attracted to Karnak when the Korr system was discovered due to its close proximity to Korrath. When the Astral Concord made first contact with the Karnaki, it was successful and beneficial to both groups. Due to this, the Karnaki became interested with Concord technology and became skilled in adapting and improving advanced technology. As such, Karnaki exported mechans and other technology to the rest of the galaxy, although this didn't last long. Once Korrath was almost abandoned by the first wave of colonizers, Karnak suffered from its distance from the Core Systems as visitors to the planet grew smaller and smaller until visitors stopped traveling to Karnak altogether. Karnaki exports were more easily met by other manufacturers closer to the Core.

Karnaki became split over the idea of working with offworlders, and with the planet's economy in ruins, wars erupted throughout the planet for millennia, while the rest of the galaxy largely forgot the planet and left it to its endless wars. A few hundred years prior to the Severite Crisis, the world was rediscovered by the Astral Concord and manufacturing deals were made to sell and distribute their advanced technology and mechans. Allying with the Forgeworks Corporation of the Forge Union, the Karnaki Hives produced protocol mechans, battle mechans, and labor mechans in concert with Forgeworks for centuries.

### The Cinder Wars

In the later years of the Astral Concord, Karnak became a major base of operations for the League of Severed Worlds, a collection of star systems that spread across the galaxy. The enormous Karnaki mechan factories began to manufacture of tens of thousands of different types of battle mechans, thus maintaining their own Karnaki mechan army. During that time, Karnak built mechan armies for large corporations such as the Commerce League. To produce the Combine's mechan armies, Forgeworks Armor Workshop had approached the Karnaki on the Combine's behalf with the offer for Karnak to establish mechan foundries, create battle mechans, and produce other automata. Ten years after the election of Velran Castigon as Supreme Praetor of the Concord, the Archduke of Karnak, Poggle the Lesser, hosted a summit of the heads of the Secession. During that meeting, the trade barons pledged their military forces to Count Maren, a defrocked Sephari Master and head of the Secession. While the Severites were ready to wage war, their plans were overheard by Orven Taskorr, a Sephari Master.
