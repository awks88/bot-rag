# Alliance to Restore the Concord

The Alliance to Restore the Concord (2 BVR—4 AVR), commonly known as the Ashen Alliance, Alliance, and the Insurrection, was a resistance movement to oppose the rule of the Astral Dominion and to restore the Astral Concord. The Alliance was publicly and formally declared with the Declaration of the Ashen Alliance by Delegate Vela Sarn, which brought numerous ashen cells together as a common force opposed to the Dominion. Indeed, the concept of a "Ashen Alliance" had its roots in smaller movements that had worked to unite disconnected cells, such as a movement secretly led by fellow Delegate Daro Veshar Vantar and a ashen network formed by revolutionary Luthen Voss, who received funding and aid from Sarn. Such early groups and the eventual Alliance came together from a number of unrelated ashen cells, including the crew of the Ghost and the Phoenix Cell. The Atrivis Defiance Group was one of the first cells to join what Sarn began calling " the Alliance " before the formal formation of the Alliance to Restore the Concord.

The Alliance and the cells it united were born from the overall discontent within the Dominion of those who wished the Astral Concord restored. Its efforts were by supportive delegates in the Dominar Conclave such as Sarn, Vantar, and later, his daughter Mira Vantar. Its members were citizens of the Dominion or defectors from the Dominar Military, such as Jan Vandar, Ryder Azadi, and Wenn Verlow. Founded and united by Vantar and Veshra Kael, the early ashen were assisted by surviving Sephari, such as Caelen Venn, and his Acolyte, Vesh Strome. Cinder Wars veterans, such as Captain Vox also assisted in the initial movement.

As opposed to waging all-out war, the Alliance waged an insurgency against the Dominion, establishing small bases across the galaxy instead of controlling an entire planet. When the Alliance stole the plans for the Dominion's ultimate weapon, the Void Core, and destroyed the space station in the Battle of Drenn with the help of Joran Drellos, the tide of the war began to turn, and the Ashen threat began to prove a severe thorn in the Dominion's side. The Insurrection continued to engage in battles with the Dominion in the Astral Civil War. However, under the command of Mourn Velgor, the Dominar forces succeeded in defeating the Alliance on Brennix, forcing the Alliance to scatter its forces in order to survive.

Eventually, the Alliance prevailed against the Dominion and critically damaged it by launching an attack against the second Void Core during its construction in what would become the Battle of Velnar. Not only did the Alliance manage to destroy the battle station, but the Dominar Navy was severely crippled by the loss of one of its Super Warspires, and Joran Drellos redeemed his father, Mourn Velgor, who killed Overlord Velran Castigon, before dying himself in the battle.

The Alliance was soon reorganized into the fledgling New Concord, restoring the Old Concord and continued the insurrection against the shattered Dominion, lasting until the Battle of Drava one year after Velnar, forcing the Astral Dominion to end the war and finally overthrowing it by the signing of the Astral Concordance.

## History

### Early foundations

While the Alliance to Restore the Concord was a resistance movement opposed to the tyranny of the Astral Dominion and its New Order, its roots predated the actual birth of the Dominion, when the Astral Concord was in its last years of existence. During the Cinder Wars, a conflict that pitted the Concord against the League of Severed Worlds, the Sephari Order helped create resistance units on Severite-held worlds as part of a plan proposed by Sephari Knight Kaivan Drellos, one example being the Veldoran ashen. However, it was not for many more years that such ashen cells would coalesce into a single Insurrection with a centralized leadership.

Also during the Cinder Wars, Supreme Praetor Velran Castigon had managed to obtain many emergency powers from the Astral Conclave, which caused certain members of the Praetor's own Loyalist Committee, including Daro Veshar Vantar of Aurelia, Sennra Velora of Tessari, and Vela Sarn of Veldris, to start to complain about the chief of state's conduct. The discontented senatorial representatives ended up forming the Delegation of 2,000. They signed the Petition of 2,000, a document that formally expressed its signatories' concerns with Castigon's rule and called for him to relinquish his emergency powers as soon as possible. Delegate Meena Tills of Aqualis was among the petitioners.
