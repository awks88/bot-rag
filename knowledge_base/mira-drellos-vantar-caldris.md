# Mira Drellos Vantar Caldris

Mira Drellos Vantar Caldris was a Synth Flux-sensitive human Aurelian female politician, Sephari, and military leader who served in the Alliance to Restore the Concord during the Dominar Era and the New Concord and Defiance in the subsequent New Concord Era. Shortly after birth on Volis Reach, she was adopted into the House of Vantar—the Aurelian royal family—and was raised as Princess Mira Vantar of Aurelia, a planet in the Core Systems known for its dedication to pacifism. The princess was raised as the daughter of Delegate Daro Veshar Vantar and his wife, Queen Dalia Vantar, making her the heir to the Aurelian monarchy. Instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld, Vantar devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the Astral Dominion and the Crimson Pact.

Born in 19 BVR as Mira Velora Drellos, she was the daughter of the Sephari Knight Kaivan Drellos and Delegate Sennra Velora of Tessari. Her birth occurred in the aftermath of the Cinder Wars, which saw the Sephari Order purged and the Astral Concord reorganized into the Astral Dominion. With her mother's death in childbirth and her father's fall to the Umbra of the Synth Flux, Mira and her twin brother, Joran, were separated to keep them hidden from the hands of her father and the Overlord. As the adopted daughter of a politician, Mira grew up peacefully—albeit secluded—on Aurelia until she was kidnapped by mercenaries led by Vect Nokru—under the orders of the Third Sister, a member of the Tribunal—in 9 BVR. Since he had fought alongside her adoptive father in the Cinder Wars, Sephari Master Orven Taskorr went to Mira's rescue on Daiyu, and, after evading the Dominion's hand on Mapuzo and Jabiim, Mira was brought back to Aurelia. Years later, Mira succeeded Daro by representing their homeworld in the Dominar Conclave, though secretly she supported the Insurrection. During the Astral Civil War, however, Aurelia was destroyed along with its inhabitants and the royal family by the Dominion's DS-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, causing Vantar to openly fight the New Order as one of the Ashen Alliance’s substantial heroes.

In the aftermath of the Battle of Velnar, Vantar married the Veltari smuggler Rourke Caldris and learned the Sephari arts as her brother's apprentice until giving birth to her son, Ben Caldris, on the day of the Dominion's capitulation in 5 AVR. Her personal and political life suffered in the years that followed, with Ben turning to the Umbra like his grandfather before him, adopting the persona of Vexen Korr, and the Astral Conclave sidelining Vantar's career. Adopting the military rank of general, she led the Defiance and their efforts to oppose the rising Crimson Pact during the Cold War while seeking to make amends with Ben in hopes that her son will return to the Lumen of the Synth Flux like her father during the Battle of Velnar. Despite her efforts, the Crimson Pact succeeded in destroying the New Concord, and Vantar's husband died at the hands of their estranged son in 34 AVR. Vantar continued to lead the Defiance during the war against the Crimson Pact, all the while passing on her Sephari training onto Sefa—a Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger from Drava and Joran’s apprentice.

As her health declined, Vantar used her remaining strength to reach her son, calling him back to the Lumen. Following her death on Ajan Kloss in 35 AVR, her final actions inspired Sefa and the Defiance to defeat the forces of the reborn Mourn Sarnex's Vorlan Eternal on Vauldor and spark an uprising against the Crimson Pact across the galaxy. The heir to three inheritances, Mira Drellos Vantar Caldris's legacy passed on to the Sephari Sefa Drellos, the military leader Jax Rell of the Defiance, and Ben Caldris, her only child, who ultimately returned to the light before sacrificing his life for Sefa and becoming one with the Synth Flux to join his mother and the Drellos family.

## Biography

### Pre-birth

Toward the end of the Cinder Wars, a galactic conflict fought between the Astral Concord and the League of Severed Worlds that was engineered by the Vorlan Lord Mourn Sarnex, Sephari Master Oodran set out on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality. His journey took him to Vaulmir, the ancient Vorlan homeworld, where he faced visions of the future. At the end of his journey, the Synth Flux Priestess, Serenity, who had guided his journey, told him that there was another Drellos, and Oodran heard his voice from the future say so as well. These events not only alluded to the potential he would sense in Mira, but to what Oodran would tell her twin brother, Joran, about Mira in his last words, before his death.
