# Veshtan

Veshtans, sometimes referred to xenophobically as tail-heads or Twi, occasionally referred to as Rylothians, were a tall near-human species whose most striking feature was a pair of long appendages protruding from their skulls, called lekku. They hailed from the planet of Veshtar, in the Far Verge Territories, and female Veshtans were often exported off world as slaves. Veshtans were considered one of the most common species in the galaxy, with its members easily able to blend into a crowd without drawing attention, as opposed to rarer, more prominent species, like Togrutas. Some armies used this to their advantage, employing Veshtans as undercover agents.

## Biology and appearance

Veshtans were a species of tall humanoids whose skin spanned a vast array of colors, including white, orange, yellow, green, pink, blue, purple, black, gray, and red. Their most distinctive feature was the pair of prehensile appendages that sprouted from the crown or base of their skulls, known as lekku, or head-tails. Although Veshtans normally had two lek, some individuals had four. Super sensitive lek could grab and hold objects and react to the slightest touch. A damaged lekku could cause serious harm to a Veshtan's health.

The species had at least two sexes, male and female. While most males typically had ears with lobes resembling those of humans, some males had cone-shaped hearing organs located in the same place on the head, a trait customarily found in the females of the same species. Although Veshtans naturally had blunt teeth, males typically sharpened them into points.

Veshtans were usually thin, although corpulent individuals were known to exist. Under normal circumstances, they generally had a higher body temperature than other humanoid species. They had even higher body temperatures when in fear.

Veshtans and humans could breed to create a hybrid of the two species: shortly after the death of Caelen Venn, Nara Velko gave birth to their son, who appeared mostly human but inherited his mother's green pigment in his hair and ears. He was born after the liberation of Vedros, in which Velko lived to participate.

## Society and culture

The Veshtans had a language of their own, called Veshtani, which combined verbal sounds and an elaborate, subtle sign language using their lekku. Veshtan's lekku could be moved to create gestures with meanings such as hello or goodbye. For example, both lekku tips crossed twice meant, "I love you." Veshtans were capable of speaking Astral Basic Standard, many with a noticeable accent; although it was possible for some to lose this accent if away from Veshtar for an extended period of time, it could still resurface. For example, Nara Velko lapsed back into the common Veshtani accent during an argument with her father and also used it consciously when passing herself off as a slave.

voidbat Mist Spire and voidbat Coronet City were spicy dishes made from mynocks that were popular with Veshtans, as well as a dish known as fried voidbat. Veshtans were eaten by gutkurrs, vicious insect carnivores that used their sharp, curved claws to snare their prey. An integral part of Veshtan culture and the familial clan was the Kalikori, an heirloom based down through the generations, each of which would add to the totem. The Kalikori was only of value to the family from which it came from and was worthless to outsiders, who had no connection to that familial line. On the Veshtan homeworld Veshtar, Veshtans celebrated the holiday called Longest Night when each of the planet's three moons were at their lowest phase. During this holiday, many would remain inside with their families as the populated hemisphere of the planet was covered in darkness.

## History

### Cinder Wars

The homeworld of the Veshtans was Veshtar, a planet of harsh extremes situated in the Far Verge Territories. That world was part of the Astral Concord, a democratic union that governed most of the galaxy. During the Cinder Wars, Veshtar was occupied by the League of Severed Worlds. Unsatisfied with the tyrannical rule of the Secession, a Veshtan Defiance movement arose, led by Varlo Velko, to combat the Severites. This movement was publicly backed and actively assisted by the Astral Concord. Working with the Grand Army of the Concord and the Sephari Order, the Veshtan Defiance managed to expel Severite forces under Wat Tambor. The Concord then annexed Veshtar.

### Dominar protectorate

Following the end of the Cinder Wars, the Concord was transformed into the Astral Dominion, and Veshtar was annexed by the Dominion. The planet became an Dominar protectorate, and the Veshtans living on Veshtar were subjected to Dominar rule. While Veshtar was nominally independent and was still represented by Delegate Orn Free Taa in the Astral Conclave, real power lay in the hands of the Dominar Warden Delian Mors.
