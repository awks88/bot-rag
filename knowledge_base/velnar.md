# Velnar

Velnar, designated IX3244-A, also known as the Forest Moon of Velnar or the Sanctuary Moon, was a small forested moon that was the ninth moon that orbited the Far Verge planet of the same name. It was the homeworld of the sentient Dulok, Tannik, and Yuzzum species, as well as the Wistie and semi-sentient Gorax races.

## Description

A remote moon in the Velnar system of the Far Verge Territories, Velnar was covered in dense woodlands, tall mountains, and savannas, and home to the sentient Tannik, Dulok, and Yuzzum species. The semi-sentient Gorax also dwelled on the moon as well as the Wistie. Some of Velnar's trees reached more than 1,000 meters in height. It contained a breathable atmosphere to humans and 8% surface water. It also had two suns: Velnar I and Velnar II.

## History

Throughout galactic history, offworld raiders occasionally came to Velnar to hunt the native Tannik population to make Tannik Jerky, which was a popular snack across the Far Verge.

During the reign of the Astral Dominion, the Dominion, deeming Velnar to be perfect for its purposes due to how remote the moon was, established an outpost on the moon that included a shield generator complex to protect the incomplete second Void Core in orbit of the moon. The Dominion occupied Velnar. In order to make way for the complex, an Tannik village was razed by Dominar troops, leading to strained Dominar—Tannik relations. As a result of the repulsorlift field emitted by the SLD-26 planetary shield generator to keep the Void Core in a stationary orbit, Velnar was prone to earthquakes, tidal imbalances, and other geological disturbances that slowly but surely began to break up the small moon. The Vorlock Initiative also established Research Station 9 on the moon.

The moon was later the site of the monumental Battle of Velnar that saw combat between the Alliance to Restore the Concord and its Tannik allies against Dominar forces who were attempting to protect the shield generator and prevent the Alliance from destroying its superweapon in orbit. After fierce combat on the forest moon, the Ashen Alliance managed not only to destroy the shield generator complex, but also the second Void Core. With the destruction of the Dominion's dreaded battle station and the death of Overlord Castigon and his enforcer, Mourn Velgor, the ashen and Tanniks held a victory celebration on the moon, one of many across the galaxy.

The Ashen set up shields and tractor beams to protect the moon from debris from the destroyed space station. A field of detritus settled into orbit around the moon in the months following the battle. Additionally, the many riftspace anomalies present in the Moddell sector absorbed the worst of the Void Core debris fallout. Despite that, some debris still rained down on the moon for a decade after the battle, but Velnar's ecosystem proved resilient.

At some point, the Tannik Civil War brought strife to Velnar. The New Concord intervened by sending V4-N7 and Captain Jaxxon T. Tumperakki on a diplomatic mission to Velnar. During the New Concord Era, Velnar remained remote and rarely-visited. In 35 AVR, following the Battle of Vauldor, a Resurgent-class Warspire stationed above the forest moon was destroyed through use of the Holdo maneuver. The event was watched by Wicket W. Warrick and his son, Pommet Warrick.
