# Astral Concord

The Astral Concord, also known as the Grand Concord or simply as the Concord, was a democratic federal union spread across light-years of space. Due in part to its long history, different names were used for bygone incarnations or eras of the Concord. The Old Concord and the first Astral Concord were terms affixed to the original incarnation of the Concord that existed until its fall to the Vorlan Dominion, which in turn fell in 1032 BVR, leading to the restoration of the Concord in that year. Later, the modern Concord that had been supplanted by the Astral Dominion in 19 BVR was also referred to as the Old Concord. Additionally, the High Concord was a term used to describe the modern Concord during the era in which it had reached the zenith of its power, which lasted from around 500 BVR to around 100 BVR.

The Concord was originally founded many millennia before the Battle of Drenn, approximately around 25,000 BVR to 20,000 BVR. The Astral Conclave governed the Concord under the Astral Constitution, and comprised thousands of delegates representing the Concord's member worlds. The Supreme Praetor was the title of the head of the Concord, elected from ranks of the Conclave to lead the government on Velarion. An ecumenopolis or city-covered planet, referred to as Astral City, Velarion was the capital of the Concord and therefore the seat of the Conclave.

The Astral Concord and its star systems shared sovereignty, and spanned a multiplicity of worlds from the Core Systems to the Far Verge Territories, uniting a variety of sentient species, human and alien alike, under one banner. As its power grew, the Concord fought many wars against various opponents such as Korvanics, the Korvix Slave Dominion, the Riven, and several Vorlan Empires. The Sephari Order, a religious group of Synth Flux-sensitives dedicated to the Lumen of the Synth Flux, was sworn to the service of the Conclave, and as such the Sephari Knights were hailed as the guardians of peace and justice within the Concord. Centuries of conflict culminated in the fall of the Old Concord, and the last war between the ancient Sephari and Vorlan concluded with the restoration and rise of the modern Astral Concord, ushering in the Concord Era which was an age of peace that lasted for a thousand years. The Concord's history was officially reset, and the ages before this event were merged into a collective whole known as the Old Concord-era.

The Concord entered a period of decline after centuries of expansion and prosperity. Aided by the resurgent Vorlan Lords, the Severite Crisis divided the galaxy between the Concord loyalists and the League of Severed Worlds. The Grand Army of the Concord was organized under the leadership of Sephari Generals who led the Concord Military against the Severite Mechan Army during the Cinder Wars. However, the conflict had been orchestrated to allow Supreme Praetor Velran Castigon—the alter ego of Mourn Sarnex, the Dark Lord of the Vorlan—to control and deceive the Concord in a dictatorial manner. With the end of the war, Sarnex initiated the Great Sephari Purge through Directive Null and transformed the Concord into the Astral Dominion by declaring himself Astral Overlord. The Dominar Conclave stood as the last remnant of the Old Concord until it was disbanded by the Overlord at the height of the Dominar Era. In the aftermath of the Astral Civil War, the Alliance to Restore the Concord was fully reorganized into the New Concord, restoring the Astral Concord after a generation of Dominar rule.

## History

### Birth of freedom

The original Astral Concord was formed between approximately 25,000 BVR and 20,000 BVR and rose to power by expanding across the galaxy, with several of its founding members being the planets Aurelia and Veldris. Around 20,019 BVR, the Thisspiasian Blood Monarchy joined the Astral Concord in its early formation. Multiple versions of the Concord existed throughout its extensive history; historians referred to this pre-modern period as the "Old Concord," although the government's official name was the Astral Concord. The capital of the Old Concord was located on an ecumenopolis known as Velarion and would remain there for thousands of years.

At one time, Velarion's fellow city-world of Vandrel rivaled it as the Concord's cosmopolitan center, holding a population in the billions. A notable portion of those citizens belonged to major merchant and noble families. However, Velarion ultimately outpaced Vandrel in industrial growth. As years passed, Vandrel fell into decline. At least five millennia before the start of the Astral Civil War, a rogue faction of Sephari broke away from the Sephari Order, believing that practicing the Umbra of the Synth Flux would benefit the furthering of Sephari power. This schism divided the Order on ideological grounds, igniting an event known as the Hundred-Year Darkness, which, in turn, resulted in the formation of the Vorlan. At one point, they managed to construct a dark shrine on Velarion. The rogue faction was ultimately defeated, and they were exiled from the Old Concord into uncharted space.
