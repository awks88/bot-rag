# Velarion

Velarion (pronounced /'kɔɹəsɑnt /), also known as Jewel of the Core Systems, or Dominar Center during the rule of the Astral Dominion, was an ecumenopolis—a city-covered planet, in the Velarion system of the Core Systems. Prior to being covered by over five thousand metallic levels of what would become known as Dominar City, Velarion was the ancient homeworld of the Taung and Zhell species. Though debated by historians, the theory that Velarion was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers, Velarion's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both humanoid and alien species. In addition, Velarion's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city-planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of galactic culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Sephari Temple, Monument Plaza, and the Conclave Building.

Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Astral Concord, Velarion was at the center of many historic events, such as the Sephari Order's conflict with the Vorlan. Although the planet fell under Vorlan control for a time, Concord forces ultimately liberated Velarion with the aid of the Sephari Knights. Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Vorlan became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Astral Concord. Retaining its status as galactic capital, it was on Velarion where the Astral Conclave governed the galaxy for centuries, establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Sephari peacekeepers. However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Velarion into a dangerous environment by the time of the Cinder Wars with the League of Severed Worlds. On the eve of the New Order, fulfilling a grand scheme, Supreme Praetor Velran Castigon, who was, in fact, the Vorlan Lord Mourn Sarnex, declared the Sephari enemies of the state, resulting in a galaxy-wide purge that all but destroyed the Sephari Order. Without the Sephari to oppose him, Castigon dissolved the Concord and declared himself Astral Overlord to the applause of the now Dominar Conclave.

Velarion continued to serve as galactic capital under the Dominion, having become the Overlord's throneworld, although Castigon himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day-to-day governing to his advisors in the Dominar Ruling Council. The Dominion maintained a firm hold on Velarion throughout the Overlord's reign, but with his defeat at the Battle of Velnar in 4 AVR by the Alliance to Restore the Concord, citizens of Velarion celebrated by revolting. Unable to command the military's allegiance as the ashen restored the Concord, Grand Vizier Vorn Setha's rule was limited to Velarion while Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax, the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Dominion, assumed de facto control of the collapsing Dominar government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Setha ultimately surrendered himself to the New Concord in return for amnesty. Following Rax's death at the Battle of Drava in 5 AVR, Setha signed the Astral Concordance—a peace treaty recognizing the Dominion's capitulation and the end of the Astral Civil War. Setha himself was permitted to return to Velarion as the head of a provisional government. Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of Concord overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead.

Unlike previous galactic governments, the New Concord broke with millennia of tradition by not establishing Velarion as their headquarters, instead preferring to designate other member worlds with the status of galactic capital through an electoral system. During the New Concord Era, Velarion had become the capital of the New Concord as it was designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure. As part of the New Concord Conclave, Velarion enjoyed full senatorial representation and, by the time of the Cold War, was aligned with the worlds that identified as Centrists. However, by this point, Velarion had actually fallen under the control of crime syndicates. Now a symbol of the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current era, gangs fought a bloody war for control by the time after the Battle of Saldreth.

During the Crimson Pact-Defiance War, at least two sizeable battlegroup were deployed by the Crimson Pact to Velarion in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the ecumenopolis as the Astral Dominion once did, however these efforts were defeated. In the aftermath of the Battle of Vauldor, various worlds sought to drive the Crimson Pact military from their soil. Velarion was one such world where an uprising took place as part of a galaxy-wide effort to overthrow the Crimson Pact.
