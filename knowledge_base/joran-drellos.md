# Joran Drellos

Joran Drellos, a Synth Flux-sensitive human male, was a legendary Sephari Master who fought in the Astral Civil War during the reign of the Astral Dominion. Along with his companions, Princess Mira Vantar and General Rourke Caldris, Drellos served as a revolutionary on the side of the Alliance to Restore the Concord—an organization committed to the downfall of the Astral Dominion and the restoration of democracy. Following the war, Drellos became a living legend, and was remembered as one of the greatest Sephari in galactic history.

The son of Sephari Knight Kaivan Drellos and Delegate Sennra Velora, Joran Drellos was born along with his twin sister, Mira, in 19 BVR. As a result of Velora's death and Kaivan's fall to the Umbra of the Synth Flux, the Drellos children were separated and sent into hiding, with Mira adopted by the royal family of Aurelia while Joran was raised by his relatives on Korrath. Longing for a life of adventure and purpose, Drellos joined the Insurrection and began learning the ways of the Synth Flux under the guidance of Sephari Master Orven Taskorr, whose first apprentice was Joran's own father. During the Battle of Drenn, Drellos saved the Alliance from annihilation by destroying the Dominion's planet-killing superweapon, the Void Core. He continued his training in the years that followed, determined to become a Sephari Knight like his father before him, and found a new mentor in Grand Master Oodran. After his master's death, Drellos participated in the Battle of Velnar in 4 AVR, during which he confronted the Vorlan Lord Mourn Velgor, whom he learned was in fact his father, Kaivan Drellos. With Joran's help, Kaivan returned to the Lumen of the Synth Flux by killing the Overlord and Dark Lord of the Vorlan Mourn Sarnex at the cost of his own life, fulfilling his destiny as the Fated One. Following the Battle of Velnar, Drellos trained his sister as a first Sephari apprentice on Ajan Kloss, but Mira ended her training after the birth of her son and seeing his death as a result of finishing her knighthood.

With the Vorlan's destruction and the subsequent capitulation of the Dominion, Drellos became a living legend as he traveled across the galaxy during New Concord Era, searching for knowledge that would aid him in rebuilding the Sephari Order, and serving the New Concord and its allies where he could. As the war raged for a year after Velnar, Drellos was heavily involved with the New Concord's efforts against self-declared Grand Warden Ubrik Adelhard in the Vandel sector, before defeating the madman at the Battle of Drava. Around 6 AVR, Drellos committed himself to the liberation of the Nagai and their homeworld Nagi from beneath the heel of their enslavers. Later, in 9 AVR, Drellos rescued Yobi, a Korvanic Synth Flux-sensitive child that belonged to the same species as Oodran, from Warden Veskar's forces, and after temporarily taking the child as an apprentice on Ossus. During the training, Drellos was visited by Kaivan's former acolyte, Veshra Kael, where they discussed Yobi and how Drellos sensed that the child's heart was not in relearning the Sephari path. After Yobi's caregiver, Vane Korrick, visited the planet bearing a gift for Yobi, Drellos offered him with a choice, accept the gift and return to the Korvanic, or take Oodran's plasma-brand and continue with his Sephari training. Yobi chose to return to Korrick, while Drellos bid Kael farewell as she departed for ways unknown.

By 15 AVR, Drellos had taken on Ben Caldris, his sister's son who inherited the family bloodline in the Synth Flux and had the potential for limitless power, as an apprentice. While both traveled to Elphrona with the explorer Lor San Tekka to study lost Sephari lore, Caldris began to be groomed by Drahl, a humanoid being powerful in the Synth Flux. Upon the planet, the trio first encountered the Knights of Korr. In 21 AVR, Drellos started feeling an increasing darkness in the Synth Flux, having dark visions that frequently came to him in meditation, and undertook a quest to find the hidden world of Vauldor. Drellos journeyed to Velkar, where he saw a vision of Vauldor and faced nine wraiths when the Synth Flux ghost of Kaivan Drellos appeared next to him. Following this, Drellos was able to defeat Kiza and the spirit of Exim Panshard but was ultimately unable to locate Vauldor after failing to track down an assassin called Ochi.
