# Plasma-brand

The plasma-brand, also referred to as a laser sword by those who were unfamiliar with it, was a weapon usually used by the Sephari, Vorlan, and other Synth Flux-sensitives. Plasma-brands had a plasma blade emitted from a usually metal hilt, and could be shut off at will or at the touch of a button. The hilt contained a power cell, like the Diatium power cell in Mourn Velgor's plasma-brand. It also contained a voidstone crystal which had been attuned to the Synth Flux by a Sephari, and which amplified the energy from the power cell to create the plasma beam, as well as containing it within a blade-like field. The energy blade had no mass, but plasma-brands operated on principles of controlled electromagnetic arc-wave energy, creating a gyroscopic effect that made them challenging to handle. It was a weapon that required skill and training, and was greatly enhanced when used in conjunction with the Synth Flux. Though also used by the Vorlan, the plasma-brand was synonymous with the Sephari, with some in the galaxy believing only Sephari could use plasma-brands.

Plasma-brands were generally used for both offense and defense. A plasma-brand could cut through virtually anything, from flesh to blast doors. The only ways to block the incoming attack of a plasma-brand was with a weapon made with material that conducted energy, such as an electrostaff and a Z6 riot control baton, resistant metals like beskar and cortosis, another plasma-brand or an ichor sword. When used defensively, a Synth Flux-sensitive could deflect pulse-caster bolts with a plasma-brand, and with skill, could even reflect the shots back toward the shooter or some other target. Experienced Sephari could even employ their plasma-brands to absorb Synth Flux lightning. Most practitioners used one single-bladed plasma-brand, though some used double-bladed plasma-brands or even multiple plasma-brands at once. Despite lacking Synth Flux-sensitivity, the cyborg General Skarl was capable of wielding four plasma-brands at once thanks to his advanced mechanical body and enhanced cybernetic brain.

Plasma-brand combat was divided into seven Forms: Form I, Form II, Form III, Form IV, Form V, Form VI, and Form VII.

## History

### Early history

Sounding like no other weapon in the galaxy, plasma-brands were used thousands of years before the age of the Astral Concord, seeing use in the Great Scourge of Vaulmor. They were also used in the Battles of Rashfond and the Peacekeeping of Parliock, The proto-saber was an ancient type of prototype plasma-brand.

### Concord Era

During the history of the Sephari Order, the plasma-brand was considered to be the Sephari's only true ally in battle. As Master Orven Taskorr once declared to his Acolyte Kaivan Drellos in the days of the late Concord, the plasma-brand was a Sephari's life. Sephari saw the plasma-brand as a symbol of the care that they would wield using their gifts given by the Synth Flux, and also served as a message to their opponents: mastering the plasma-brand required great skill and training. The choice of the saber was also the symbol of their role and duty as protectors, not destroyers, and the fact that the Sephari did not seek power and only sought to stand tall against darkness. Still, Sephari knew they would need to be more than their plasma-brands.

When Acolyte Enya Keen lost her plasma-brand during a mission to Dalna, her master, Char-Ryl-Roy, reminded her that she would need to act as a Sephari without the weapon. While finding a voidstone crystal was still a sacred part of a Sephari's duty and Keen felt like losing her plasma-brand would feel like losing part of herself, she willingly gave away the crystal if it meant finding more about the Path of the Open Hand and its plans. During the sabotage of the Steady Wing in 232 BVR, Acolyte Imri Cantaros dealt with emotional struggles that lead to his plasma-brand beam turning transparent and weak. Plasma-brands took on a more prominent role in galactic affairs as the Cinder Wars came to pass. Sephari, previously peacekeepers, became Sephari Generals of the Grand Army of the Concord, and thus began using their plasma-brands more often than they had in their role of peacekeeping.

### Dominar Era

For the majority of the Dominion's reign, the only plasma-brands that were prominently used were those of Mourn Velgor and the Arbiters who served him. All the same, there were still plasma-brands used by Sephari survivors and those they trained, like Orven Taskorr, Joran Drellos, Veshra Kael, Caelen Venn, and Vesh Strome. Velgor wielded a dual-phase plasma-brand, a design of an older, more dangerous generation. Domina Vornel of the Vornel Corporation tried to recreate the plasma-brand through the Vornel protoblade.

### New Concord Era

After the Dominion was defeated, Joran began training a new generation of Sephari. Kael adopted the Korvanic Tessa Wren as her Acolyte, with Wren first taking on the Voidblade before becoming Veshra's acolyte, then after becoming Ahsokas Acolyte she took on Strome's plasma-brand as her own. In the wake of the conflict on Peridea, both ended up stranded in the far galaxy as Grand Admiral Mitth'raw'nuruodo "Vaskorr" had returned. Strome, with a new plasma-brand in his possession, did manage to return to the galaxy as well, however.
