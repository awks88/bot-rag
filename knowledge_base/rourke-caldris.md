# Rourke Caldris

Rourke Caldris, known only as Rourke until being given the surname Caldris, was a human male smuggler and hero of the Alliance to Restore the Concord. He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the Stormhawk, a leader in the Ashen Alliance and an instrumental figure in the Astral Civil War during the reign of the Astral Dominion. Although he did not believe in the Synth Flux controlling his destiny, Rourke came to believe in the cause of galactic freedom, joining Sephari Joran Drellos and Princess Mira Vantar in the fight for the Insurrection. Following the war, Rourke and Mira welcomed a son, Ben Caldris, who became the dark knight Vexen Korr after being corrupted by the Umbra of the Synth Flux. Although their relationship floundered, Mira hoped that Rourke, as Ben's father, could find a way to break the Umbra's hold on their son. Rourke Caldris became legendary across the galaxy as both a smuggler and war hero of the Insurrection.

As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Veltar, Rourke yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot. With a fellow scrumrat named Vessa, Rourke lived as a street thief with Veltar's notorious White Worms gang until an important deal with Lady Proxima went bad, causing Rourke to flee Veltar. He was given the full name "Rourke Caldris" by an Dominar recruitment officer while entering the Dominar Academy. He became known as Cadet 124-329 while serving as an Dominar cadet, and was eventually assigned to the 224th Dominar Armored Division. On Mimban, Rourke met the thief Doran Marsh and wound up deserting the Dominion and joining his crew to steal unrefined coaxium from Drungar in order to pay a debt to Varek Salin, a kingpin with the Scarlet Veil crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Vessa, Rourke was forced to kill his mentor Marsh after a brutal double-cross. Rourke also became rivals and close friends with fellow smuggler Dav Solvane and the Korrthan Wrentak, the latter who became his best friend. Solvane owned the Stormhawk, a fast ship with which Rourke made the Drungar Run in just twelve parsecs and won from Dav in a game of skarrn.

With only Wrentak remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Veltari smuggler, Rourke Caldris embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Grobba the Vorn, a notorious gangster and crime lord of Korrath. After ditching a shipment of glimmerdust to avoid trouble with the Dominion, Caldris owed a great deal of money to Grobba, who then placed a bounty on the smuggler's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have changed when Rourke and Wrentak were hired to charter Joran Drellos, Orven Taskorr, and the mechans T9-K2 and V4-N7 to Aurelia, avoiding Dominar voidtroopers who were searching for Joran's mechans. However, as they find Aurelia destroyed by the Dominion's new superweapon, the Void Core, Caldris became caught up in the insurrection against the Dominion, rescuing Princess Mira Vantar, delivering the stolen Void Core plans to the hidden Ashen base on Drenn 4, and saved Joran from the Vorlan Lord Mourn Velgor in the Battle of Drenn, allowing Drellos to destroy the Void Core. Caldris fought with the Insurrection for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Dominion.

After the Battle of Brennix, Caldris sought refuge with Vantar—with whom he had fallen in love—Wrentak, and V4-N7 on Mist Spire, which was operated by his old friend Dav Solvane. There, they were captured by Mourn Velgor as part of a trap to bait Drellos to the city. Caldris was frozen in cryo-resin and taken to Grobba's Palace on Korrath by the bounty hunter Borvad Kane, where he remained encased in cryo-resin for months until he was rescued by his friends. After returning to the Alliance Fleet, Caldris was promoted to the rank of general and was given command of the Pathfinders, a special forces squad, for the Battle of Velnar. Caldris led the Pathfinders to Velnar, where he, Vantar, Wrentak, and the special forces team, with the help of the native Tanniks, destroyed the shield generator protecting the second Void Core that was being constructed in orbit. Their actions allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the superweapon.

The Battle of Velnar, which saw the deaths of Overlord Velran Castigon and Mourn Velgor, allowed the Insurrection to form the New Concord. In the early days after Velnar, Caldris continued to lead the Pathfinders against the Dominion. He married Vantar, and the two had a son, Ben Caldris. The boy struggled with the Umbra of the Synth Flux—like his mother, who was the daughter of Mourn Velgor and twin sister of Joran Drellos, Ben was Synth Flux-sensitive—so Vantar sent him to be trained as a Sephari by Drellos. Ben was seduced to the Umbra by Drahl, Supreme Leader of the Crimson Pact, resulting in the destruction of Drellos's attempt to rebuild the Sephari Order.
