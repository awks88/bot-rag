# Cindara

Cindara was a young, small planet located in the Cindara system, within the Atravis sector of the galaxy's Far Verge Territories. Once a garden world nourished by the Bright Star artifact, its orbit was shifted when Lady Corvax unleashed the energies of the Bright Star in an attempt to return her husband to life. The resulting gravimetric duel between the gas giants Jestefad and Lefrani over Cindara heated the planet's core, transforming the lush world into an imbalanced volcanic hellscape. Adapting to life on their devastated homeworld, the arthropodal Cindarans evolved into two distinct subspecies that cooperated to ensure their continued survival. Legends of Corvax's search for immortality brought the ancient Vorlan to Cindara, seeking the same secrets of eternal life. There, they built a temple over a locus in the Umbra of the Synth Flux, above the buried ruins of Corvax Fortress.

Acquired by the Forge Union over three hundred years before the Cinder Wars, Cindara's lava flows were mined for unique and valuable minerals. It was also home to the Umbral Cartel crime syndicate, whose headquarters was located near the mining town that became known as Zitontown after the Falleen Ziton Moj ascended to leadership of the organization. As part of the League of Severed Worlds during the Cinder Wars, the Forge Union on Cindara produced battle mechans for the Severite Mechan Army. Mourn Sarnex had a secret facility on the planet where he intended to turn " harvested " Synth Flux-sensitive children into Synth Flux-talented spies. However, the Vorlan Lord had the base scuttled when Sephari Knight Kaivan Drellos and his Acolyte, Veshra Kael, arrived to rescue the novices.

Cindara served as the final redoubt of the Severite Council until the newly-appointed Mourn Velgor, formerly Kaivan Drellos, was sent by Sarnex to slaughter the council, effectively ending the Cinder Wars. The Vorlan Lord was confronted by his wife, Sennra Velora, and his former Sephari Master, Orven Taskorr. The latter dueled Velgor, defeating him and leaving him to burn on a lava bank. However, Velgor was saved when Sarnex arrived and carried him offworld. Cindara then came under the ownership of the Astral Dominion after the Forge Union and its assets were nationalized. Sarnex had new facilities built on the planet, where more Synth Flux-sensitive infants were taken and transformed into agents of the Tribunal.

During this era, Velgor returned to the site of his greatest defeat and, with the help of the ancient Vorlan Lord Mourn Momin, erected the massive monolith Fortress Velgor to serve as his personal sanctum, built on the site of the former Vorlan temple. Throughout the Age of the Dominion, Velgor used the planet to fulfill his master's biddings, with the fiery world becoming the final resting place for victims of the Great Sephari Purge. In addition, Velgor also undertook personal business within his new realm, attempting to wield the dark powers of Cindara to bring his deceased wife back from the dead.

Following the Dark Lord of the Vorlan, the Dominion took hold of the planet. Resource extraction efforts on Cindara were taken over by the Mining Guild, although strict Dominar travel restrictions on the system made it difficult for the guild to turn a profit and mining operations eventually ceased entirely. Umbral Cartel also abandoned Cindara for a more low profile world when the Dominion established a garrison there. The Cindarans resisted the Dominar occupation of their homeworld and attempted to drive them from Cindara by launching multiple assaults on Fortress Velgor.

Although it hosted one of the earliest ashen victories of the Astral Civil War, Cindara fell into obscurity following Velgor's demise at the Battle of Velnar, though Umbral Cartel did eventually return to the planet. After the destruction of the Bright Star, the planet's ecosystem slowly began to heal from the devastation it had endured for centuries prior. Many devout pilgrims journeyed to the ruins of Fortress Velgor, hoping to tap into the late Vorlan's dark power. Decades after Velgor's death, his grandson, Vexen Korr, journeyed to the world to continue the legacy of his family's grisly past. The Supreme Leader of the Crimson Pact slaughtered the Vorlan cult that defended Fortress Velgor and discovered his grandfather's Vorlan wayfinder, a tool that allowed him to locate the phantom overlord Sarnex on Vauldor.

## Description

### World of fire

Cindara was a young rocky planet within the eponymous system of the Atravis sector, in the Far Verge Territories. It was a small world situated in grid square L-19 of the Standard Astral Grid, and it lay on the Tosste Spur trade route, off the Rimma Trade Route. A part of the Western Reaches, Cindara was located 53,000 light-years from the galaxy's Core.

With an overlapping orbit, Cindara was the third orbit of its system around the primary star Priate, completing a full rotation every 36 standard hours and a revolution around its sun every 412 local days, the equivalent of 618 standard days. It had a diameter of 4,200 kilometers, or 2,610 miles, and was a terrestrial world classed as a wasteland during the Dominar Era.
