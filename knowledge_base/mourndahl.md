# Mourndahl

Mourndahl was a remote, unexplored planet in the Mourndahl system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within the Synth Flux. A remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for Sephari Grand Master Oodran during his exile after the destruction of the Sephari Order. Joran Drellos received advanced training in the ways of the Synth Flux under Sephari Master Oodran on Mourndahl, and it was later the place of Oodran's death and transformation into the Synth Flux.

## Description

Mourndahl was a planet located in the Sluis sector of the Far Verge Territories. It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life, with the Tash species and numerous creatures—such as bogwings, dragonsnakes, butcherbugs, sleens, vine snakes, and swamp slugs—living on world. Sephari Master Oodran would also make a home for himself on Mourndahl during his exile. Mourndahl was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, swampy planet, mostly covered in swamps and bogs, interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple living caves. The continental and oceanic crusts of Mourndahl were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the lahdia plant, the yoghurt plant, gnarltree, and yarum seed. The planet was devoid of any cities.

Among the purest locations in the entire galaxy within the energy field known as the Synth Flux, it shared a mysterious time-related quality with such locales as Mortis and Velmora. The Cave of Evil was a vergence aligned with the Umbra of the Synth Flux on Mourndahl. The Cave was able to expand its influence across Mourndahl until Oodran's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the Lumen of the Synth Flux that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Dominar Era, Mourndahl's ecosystem saw balance until Oodran's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Mourndahl once more.

Mourndahl had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more.

## History

### Concord Era

During the High Concord Era, an academic paper was written that described Mourndahl as a world strong in the Synth Flux.

During the Concord Era, Halka Four-Den Alpha Team was dispatched to the planet Mourndahl in order to chart and map the extending borders of the Astral Concord and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three months, Four-Den and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing plants. No trace of the team was discovered.

Near the end of the Cinder Wars, Sephari Grand Master Oodran traveled to the planet Mourndahl after hearing the voice of deceased Sephari Master Veyron Dask. He ventured into the swamp, where he communed with Dask, who appeared manifested as a group of fireflies. He told Oodran of how to communicate with the living after death through the connection between the Living Synth Flux and the Cosmic Synth Flux. Then, Oodran entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Sephari fighting the vatborn and then the mysterious Vorlan Lord.

### Dominar Era

After his failure to defeat the Vorlan Lord Mourn Sarnex following the formation of the Astral Dominion, Oodran exiled himself to Mourndahl. Using an E3-standard starship lifeboat, he began his exile, where he continued his studies of the Synth Flux. The lifeboat served as Oodran's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Mourndahl's swampy environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Oodran built a new home for himself. He positioned it near to the Umbra cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years. However, the Mourndahl wet season created a need for the Sephari Master to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.

At some point, former Sava Madelin Sun arrived on Mourndahl in search of Oodran. After saving her from a monster, Oodran used the Synth Flux to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Oodran watched from afar.
