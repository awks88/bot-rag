# Tessari

Tessari was a bountiful planet in the Chommell sector of the Mid Verge, in the Trailing Sectors and close to the border of the Far Verge Territories. It was home to the indigenous Naari species and to a population of humans known as the Tessari. Tessari was pushed to the forefront of galactic politics as the birthplace of the Dark Lord of the Vorlan Velran Castigon, who served as its representative in the Conclave of the Astral Concord.

Under the guise of his Vorlan title Mourn Sarnex, Castigon secretly engineered an invasion of his peaceful homeworld by the Commerce League, a megacorporation in a bid for power. Ostensibly done in protest over proposed taxation of formerly free trade routes, the Combine's invasion led to an outpouring of support from the Conclave for both Castigon and the newly-elected Queen of Tessari, Sennra Velora. Velora led a successful resistance movement and removed the Combine from her world during the Battle of Tessari. Castigon subsequently rose to the office of the Supreme Praetor of the Concord, with Velora eventually taking his place as the planet's senatorial representative.

Tessari would commit itself firmly to democracy in the intervening years, staying loyal to the Concord during the pan-galactic Cinder Wars, which saw several military engagements against the League of Severed Worlds come to the planet. By the time of the war's end, Castigon had amassed considerable power and proclaimed himself the head of a new Astral Dominion. Castigon's reign would eventually be ended by the Alliance to Restore the Concord, a ashen movement led by Velora's children, Mira Vantar and Joran Drellos.

Despite his origins on the planet, Castigon did not care at all for his homeworld and, following his death, Tessari was named a priority target for Operation: Cinder, a protocol that the Vorlan Lord had put in place to destroy Dominar worlds should they fail to protect their Overlord. Through the intervention of Vantar and Queen Sosha Soruna, however, Tessari was spared from ruin and went on to become a member of the successor state to the Alliance, the New Concord. Decades later, the planet would again fight against tyranny, rising up against the military junta of the Crimson Pact during its war with Vantar's Defiance.

## Description

Tessari was a small pastoral world in the Mid Verge, located near the border of the Far Verge Territories, 34,000 light-years from the Core Systems. Unlike most other worlds, the ancient planet lacked a molten core, and instead comprised a conglomerate of large, rocky bodies that made up a network of tunnels and flooded caves. The native Naari, who built their homes in the waters of Tessari, so deep that they went right through the planet, which they rarely ventured into the core, fearing the ravenous sea beasts which resided in areas such as the Caves of Eleuabad. Despite this, certain Naari navigators utilized time-honored trade routes through the planet's structure, which served as the most expedient avenues to reach other areas of the planet. Tessari's interior was rich with plasma, a naturally-occurring energy unique to the planet, and this coupled with its lack of molten core gave Tessari a reputation as an enigma to astrophysicists, who regarded the world's structure as an extremely rare phenomenon in the galaxy.

Tessari's surface comprised a vast array of different landscapes, from rolling plains and grassy hills to swampy lakes caused by the water-filled network of deep-sea tunnels. The swamps acted as a gateway to the world's seas, where legendary creatures dwelt. Beside its natural features, Tessari was considered a world of classical beauty due to the aesthetics of its population centers. The porous crust's natural plasma was harvested for energy and building material, and was generally thought to be the key to many of the planet's secrets.

While most of Tessari's land animals were peaceful, as exemplified by the gentle shaak, the planet's oceans, which held an abundance of life, teemed with menacing creatures, such as the opee sea killer, the sando aqua monster, and the colo claw fish.

## History

### Early history

Originally, Tessari was home to the amphibian Naari. The earliest offworlders to arrive were a Reptilian species known as the Elders who warred against the Naari. Eventually, the Elders vanished as mysteriously as they arrived, leaving only stone statues behind.

Approximately around 4000 BVR, Grizmallti people had discovered the planet, which they had named Nabu, after one of their principal deities. Tessari quickly became notorious as a vacation spot and as a private hunting preserve amongst big-game hunters across the Mid Verge, although it would be around a century before permanent human settlement. Initially, the Naari retreated underwater and avoided the colonists and new interlopers.
