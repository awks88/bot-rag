# Jaren Kane

Jaren Kane was a famed Korvanic human male bounty hunter and the vatborn template of the Grand Army of the Concord. Known as the best bounty hunter in the galaxy during the final years of the Astral Concord, Kane was proficient in marksmanship as well as unarmed combat. The Korvanic armor that he wore featured various weapons and gadgets, including a flamethrower, dual WESTAR-34 pulse-caster pistols, and a jetpack. His personal starship was the Vornclaw-31 -class patrol and attack craft Slave I.

Hailing from the planet Concord Dawn, Kane was a foundling of the Korvanics trained by a mentor known partially as Jaste, and, as a Korvanic warrior, fought in the Korvanic Civil Wars before becoming a bounty hunter. As a bounty hunter, Kane worked with and became acquainted with several fellow hunters and individuals in the criminal underworld including the Velmari Rezz Drannor, whom Kane once mentored, Nyx Vale, Tarsa Brixx, and the infamous pirate Brakka Varn. In time, Kane became widely regarded as the galaxy's best bounty hunter, with a notoriety matched by few other bounty hunters.

Shortly after the Invasion of Tessari, the Vorlan Lord Mourn Sevrin hired Kane to serve as the genetic foundation of an army secretly bred on the Wildreach world of Tholos. His DNA spawned millions of vatborn troopers who shared his face, voice, and were trained to become soldiers. As part of his payment, Kane was given an unaltered vatborn whom he raised as his son, Borvad Kane. In the last days of the Severite Crisis, Kane was involved in the failed plot to assassinate Delegate Sennra Velora on Velarion. The Sephari Order investigated the incident, forcing Kane to leave the Tholosian homeworld after he was confronted by Sephari Knight Orven Taskorr.

Taskorr tracked Kane to Karnak where he discovered the bounty hunter's connection with Count Maren and the League of Severed Worlds. Kane fought against the Sephari assault team during the First Battle of Karnak, and was beheaded by Sephari Master Dovan Krell in 22 BVR. His legacy survived not only through the millions of vatborn troopers that formed the backbone of the Concord Military, but also Borvad, who became a bounty hunter like his father before him. By the era of the Astral Dominion, Borvad possessed the same set of Korvanic armor worn by Jaren, although its appearance had been altered significantly.

## Biography

### Early life

Jaren Kane was born in 66 BVR and was from the Korvanic colony planet Concord Dawn, a world within the Korvan sector of the Far Verge Territories. Although he told others he came from this Korvanic world, his exact history remained unknown, much to Kane's enjoyment. As a boy, Kane was a prodigy in unarmed combat. Following the murder of his parents, Kane was found on Concord Dawn by and taken into ranks of the Korvanics as a foundling. The legendary warriors raised him to become a Korvanic warrior. He was taught by a mentor known partially as Jaste.

Kane participated in the Korvanic Civil Wars, and it was during the wars that he was first given his own set of distinctive Korvanic armor. Eventually, however, his time with the Korvanics led him to pursue a career as a bounty hunter. Although he would retain his Korvanic armor, Prime Minister Almec of the New Korvanics disavowed any connection to Kane, claiming that he was simply a bounty hunter who had somehow stolen an artifact from Korvan's troubled past.

### Rise of Jaren Kane

During the final years of the Astral Concord, but still many years prior to the Invasion of Tessari, Kane, over time, rose to the top of the bounty hunting trade, where he expertly used his advanced armor and twin pulse-caster pistols to hunt down fugitives. Initially, Kane was not able to just take on the jobs that he wanted, and it took him many years to build his reputation until he could operate as he chose to and saw fit. Ultimately, Kane became widely regarded as the best bounty hunter in the galaxy. At some point in his career Kane began to work with Tarsa Brixx as a frequent accomplice. Kane was also an associate of pirate captain Brakka Varn and fellow bounty hunter Nyx Vale. Once being mentored by the legendary man, Rezz Drannor, a Velmari who was a major bounty hunter by the time of the Cinder Wars, came to owe Kane several favors that he never paid off.

At one point in time, Kane and Brixx journeyed to a terrestrial astronomical object called Snugano. While there, Kane and Brixx used a tactic, with his companion nearly dying using her abilities as a Clawdite to morph into a fur-covered beast. At another point, Kane undertook another job to Oovo 4, a prison moon that Vornclaw attack craft were used to guard. During this event, Kane stole a Vornclaw-31 starship, naming it Slave I, utilizing it as his personal transport, and upgrading the craft extensively, outfitting the vessel with a number of weapons, including laser cannons, projectile launchers, and seismic charges.
