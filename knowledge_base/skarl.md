# Skarl

Skarl, born Varnek dai Drevok, was a Vorkhan male warlord who served within the military forces of the League of Severed Worlds as a commanding officer during the Cinder Wars in the final years of the Concord Era. In addition to his position as a general within the Severite Mechan Army, the Vorkhan held the title of Supreme Martial Commander of the Severite Mechan Armies throughout the war, where he led countless engagements against the Concord's Grand Army. Recognized for his ruthless tactics and extensive cybernetic enhancements, Skarl utilized the Severite Mechan Army and his adept combat skills to instill fear throughout the galaxy as he traveled to and invaded planets while engaging numerous Knights of the Sephari Order, his sworn enemies.

A native of Vorkha, a planet within Wildreach, Drevok grew to become a fearsome warrior and warlord amongst his people and gained an aptitude for warfare and battle tactics during Vorkhan's ruthless war against the Threxian, an insectoid species from the invading neighboring planet Threx in the star system with the same name near theirs. After demonstrating his talents and earning a bloody reputation as a warlord on his homeworld, Varnek jai Drevok eventually adopted the name "Skarl," instilling terror in the hearts of his adversaries. Skarl fought alongside Vorkha's best warriors, the Vakari, and assembled them as his bodyguards, with each serving as a khan commanding their own troops. Skarl then waged war against the Threxian with increased intensity and determination while on the offensive, as the Vorlan Lords Count Maren and Mourn Sarnex, his Vorlan master, recognized his promise as he led his people in a war against the Threxian. The Threxian were successfully expelled by the Vorkhan forces under Skarl's leadership to their own world, and Skarl launched a series of campaigns on Threx itself, advancing across the Threx worlds. However, the Astral Concord and Sephari Order intervened in the conflict on the side of the Threxian, leading to punishment and famine for the Vorkhan and generating an enduring grudge in Skarl. The Vorlan, who had significant plans for Skarl, offered him cybernetic enhancements, which the Vorkhan accepted and took on over time. Sometime later, Maren conspired to mortally wound Skarl and sabotaged his shuttle, which resulted in a gruesome crash that left the man on the brink of death. After that, Skarl gave up almost everything that remained of his organic form—whether lost in the crash or by his own decision-making—to survive.

The reconstructed Skarl became a skilled plasma-brand duelist under Maren's guidance, who trained the Vorkhan in the arts of plasma-brand combat and eventually welcomed him into a position as a warrior in the Secession. Shortly after the advent of the Cinder Wars, Skarl was quickly granted the rank of general, as well as the title of Supreme Martial Commander of the Severite Mechan Armies, making him one of the most powerful people within the Severite Alliance, second only to his master Maren. Skarl wielded his skills as a brilliant military strategist and melee combatant during numerous campaigns against the Concord throughout the Cinder Wars while also gaining a reputation for hunting and killing Sephari Knights as a Sephari hunter. He made a habit of claiming the plasma-brands of defeated Sephari as trophies and weapons to be used against other opponents. His position and threat level led to his preeminence as one of the Concord's primary targets throughout the course of the war. As the war played out, Skarl found a personal nemesis in Sephari Master Orven Taskorr, whom he dueled on numerous occasions.

Days before the end of the war in 19 BVR, Skarl, under orders from Mourn Sarnex, launched a daring and unprecedented assault on the planet Velarion, the capital world of the Concord. In his assault, the Vorkhan managed to take the Concord's Supreme Praetor, Velran Castigon—in reality secretly Sarnex himself—captive aboard his flagship, the Providence-class Dreadnought Invisible Hand. However, during the battle, the Praetor was rescued from the general's grasp by Sephari Generals Orven Taskorr and Kaivan Drellos, an engagement that also resulted in Maren's death. Subsequently, Skarl became the de facto leader of the entire Severite Alliance and immediately withdrew of all remaining reinforcements and fled into the Far Verge Territories, prolonging the war. Following the Battle of Velarion, Skarl, succeeding Maren as the Secession's Head of State, retreated to the planet Velkoth, where he addressed and relocated the members of the Executive Severite Council to the planet Cindara under Sarnex's orders. However, unknown to the general, Skarl's location on Velkoth was betrayed by Sarnex, resulting in a Concord invasion of the planet in an effort to bring a decisive conclusion to the war. Skarl was ultimately slain by his nemesis, General Taskorr, following a prolonged chase between the two, which resulted in the war's conclusion as well as the Secession's systematic collapse soon after.
