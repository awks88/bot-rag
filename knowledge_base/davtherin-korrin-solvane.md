# Davtherin Korrin Solvane

Davtherin Korrin Solvane, otherwise known as Dav Solvane, was a human smuggler, gambler, and entrepreneur who became Baron Administrator of Mist Spire and, later, a general in the Ashen Alliance. Born on Soltari, he was the owner of the Stormhawk before losing it to Rourke Caldris in a game of skarrn on Numidian Prime. Afterward, Solvane put an end to his days as a smuggler and became an entrepreneur, setting up a small mining operation on the planet Vedros before eventually becoming the leader of Mist Spire in the skies of the planet Velhaven.

During the Astral Civil War, Mourn Velgor arrived on Mist Spire to lay a trap for his son, Joran Drellos. As part of the trap, the Dark Lord of the Vorlan forced Solvane into tricking a group of Ashen, including Caldris and Princess Mira Vantar, leading them to Velgor himself. Though Velgor promised to leave Mist Spire without an Dominar presence, Solvane felt the deal had been altered to the point where he could no longer tolerate it. Solvane alerted his citizens to the Astral Dominion's presence and impending occupation and ordered an evacuation. He helped the Ashen try to rescue Caldris, who had been frozen in cryo-resin, from Borvad Kane, but the bounty hunter escaped.

Solvane joined the Ashen Alliance and set out to find Caldris. After locating him in the palace of Grobba the Vorn on Korrath, Solvane aided in his rescue. The Ashen returned to the fleet, and Solvane became a general, volunteering to lead the assault on the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station during the Battle of Velnar. During the battle, he piloted the Stormhawk into the battle station's core, firing the shot which destroyed it.

In the years of peace after the Dominion's dissolution, Solvane started a family and fathered a daughter named Kadara Solvane. However, while she was only an infant, his daughter was kidnapped by the Crimson Pact. Six years later, he embarked on a quest to the desert planet of Velsaan with Joran Drellos to find answers about the growing darkness in the Synth Flux, but failed to uncover anything. Seeking to bury the pain of those memories, Solvane settled on Velsaan and led a solitary lifestyle where he was known as the " Hermit " to the Aki-Aki of the Lurch Canyon. He lived alone for some years while keeping contact with the Caldris family and was later asked by Vantar's Defiance for his aid in the war against the Crimson Pact.

In 35 AVR, Dav helped the Defiance and reunited with his old friend, Wrentak. Solvane played one more role in the fight for galactic freedom as he brought an assembled Citizens' Fleet to reinforce the Defiance at the battle at Vauldor, from which the Defiance emerged victorious.

## Biography

### Deals and adventures

A member of the tragic Solvane family, Davtherin Korrin Solvane, otherwise known as "Dav" Solvane, was born on the planet Soltari. During his youth, he became a smuggler and a gambler, excelling in a card game known as skarrn. He traveled throughout the galaxy and piloted a freighter called the Stormhawk, which he won through a game of skarrn at the gas giant Velhaven. The ship saved his life on numerous occasions. Sometime before 10 BVR, Dav won a subtropical moon in the Oseon Belt during a game of skarrn. Dav also developed a partnership with L3-37, a custom self-made piloting mechan.

During his time with L3, Dav nearly lost her to a Vorn after making a bad bet in a game of skarrn on Gonda. At one point, Solvane was involved in an incident with a Dawinian and their companion. He also came to meet the Vennika sisters, even showing Brea Vennika how to work the controls on the Stormhawk. At another point, Brea and Senni Vennika made a mess of the room they were staying in on the city-world of Velarion in order to get back at Solvane. At some point, Solvane met a mechan mechanic who worked on the Mid Verge planet Korvis, with that mechanic coming to owe Solvane a favor.

Sometime prior to the Battle of Drenn, Dav and L3-37 were ambushed in the Halthor sector when an Dominar interdictor dropped out from riftspace. As a result of the ambush, Solvane lost a majority of his shipment of walkers that he acquired from an Er'Kit arms dealer in the Noonian sector. Dav also had dealings with Scarlet Veil on Velusia. He completed his debt to Scarlet Veil and its leader, Varek Salin. At another point, Solvane visited Pantora and successfully convinced Dominar Vice Grand Administrator Prita Sven to let him leave the planet with a freighter full of poached forlyn carcasses.
