# Aurelia

Aurelia, located in the Core Systems, was a terrestrial planet covered with mountains. During the waning decades of the Astral Concord, it was ruled by Queen Dalia Vantar and represented in the Astral Conclave by her husband, Delegate Daro Veshar Vantar. Aurelia was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.

Following the rise of Velran Castigon's Astral Dominion, Aurelia played a pivotal role in the establishment of the Ashen Alliance, a movement that sought to restore the Old Concord's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the Alliance, the planet was destroyed when Grand Warden Verrin Vorlock decided to test the superlaser of the Void Core, a moon-sized battle station developed by the Dominion, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was Princess Mira Vantar, adopted daughter of Daro and Dalia Vantar and heiress to the royal house of Aurelia.

## Description

From space, the planet of Aurelia, located in the star system of the same name within the galaxy's Core Systems, appeared as a blue-green orb enveloped in a white web of clouds. Its surface had vast bodies of water and was covered in snow-capped mountains, with patches of green grassy hills. Aurelia was known to the galaxy as "the planet of beauty." Alderaanians were very artistic and lived for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten crevasses. Famous natural landmarks of Aurelia included the Cloudshape Falls, the Glarus Lagoons, and the Isatabith rain forest.

A variety of grapevines grew on Aurelia, which notably allowed to produce Toniray and emerald wine. The local fauna included the Aurelian wolf-cat, nerfs, and a species of white-winged bird.

The cities on Aurelia were carefully designed to respect the natural beauty of their environment. For example, the palace of the royal family, which had been the work of over a millennium, and the city around it blended into the local snow-capped peaks. Aurelian architecture made use of white synthstone, which inspired the architecture of certain mining economies of the Far Verge Territories, like the floating colony of Mist Spire, and the planets Vedros and Garel.

Despite the lyrics of the traditional Aurelian lullaby " Mirrorbright " referencing a moon, the planet never possessed any natural satellites.

## History

### The Old Concord

The peaceful world of Aurelia was among the oldest members of the Astral Concord, the democratic union that governed most of the galaxy, and was among the worlds that originally founded it. Upon the formation of its monarchy it was established that the heir apparent to Aurelia must complete a Challenge of the Heart, Challenge of the Mind and Challenge of the Body which would be announced on their Day of Demand and upon completion they would be officially invested as heir. In ancient times, Aurelian heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room. Before they permanently settled on Velarion, the Sephari High Council was known to have a council on Aurelia. Aurelia at one point had a neo-classical period, which produced unique architecture. The Eastwind Manor of House Veshar was built in the neo-classical style.

Figuring that she would like the world, Yana Ro wished to visit Aurelia after the Night of Sorrow and finally breaking her ties with the Path of the Open Hand cult. Ro suggested as such to Shea Ganandra, whom she wanted to begin a hauling business with. During the High Concord Era, Aurelia was a prosperous world and, as a signatory of the Concord Defense Coalition treaties, was required to lend starships and personnel to be commanded by Concord military officers during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to Aurelia after the crisis ended. Aurelia was called upon to fulfill this obligation in 232 BVR, to aid the Concord in fighting the Riven, a group of marauders who caused the Great Riftspace Disaster.

At some point between 40 BVR and 28 BVR, the heir apparent of Aurelia, sixteen year old Princess Dalia Vantar, took part in her Day of Demand where she announced her challenges of the heart, mind and body. However during her Challenge of the Body Dalia fell from the famous Alderaaian mountain Appenza Peak and would have died had it not been for the quick action of the guards watching from a distance. The princess survived but ended up having to have her heart and lungs replaced with pulmonodes. After her fall Dalia was officially invested as crown princess and heir to the throne of Aurelia. Years later the people would still speak of Dalia's accident.

Dalia Vantar eventually succeeded her mother as queen of Aurelia.
