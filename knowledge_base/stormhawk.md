# Stormhawk

The Stormhawk, originally designated YT 492727ZED and formerly known as the Stellar Envoy, and registered or known under various aliases and pseudonyms including Broken Bell, Corell's Pride, Close Shave, Fickle Flyer, Foul Matter, Graphite Skipper, Jin-Den Smoke, Longshot, Lost and Found, Star Princess, Sunfighter Franchise, Sweet Suprise, Meetyl's Misery, Wayward Son, and Victory Ring, was a Veltari YT-1300 light freighter built around 60 BVR. Earning a legendary status under their ownership, the ship was most famously used by the smugglers Rourke Caldris and Wrentak before, during, and following the Astral Civil War.

During the Stormhawk's time in the possession of the smuggler Dav Solvane, it accumulated numerous modifications in the name of convenience and luxury, including painted exterior paneling and a large escape pod. However, during Rourke Caldris's famous record-breaking Drungar Run, in which he piloted the freighter for the first time, many of these additions were destroyed or lost, leaving the ship battered by the time Caldris won it from Solvane shortly afterwards in a game of skarrn. Its aged appearance still belied numerous advanced modifications to boost the ship's speed, weapons, and shields, including a riftdrive engine among the fastest in the entire galaxy, enabling it to outrun Dominar Warspires. It also included sensor-proof smuggling compartments, which were used during the rescue of Princess Mira Vantar in 1 BVR to evade Dominar voidtroopers. Afterwards, the Stormhawk saw further action when Caldris chose to join the Ashen Alliance during the Battle of Drenn, where it was able to sneak up and fire on Mourn Velgor's Wraith Advanced, allowing Joran Drellos to fire the plasma lances from his T-65B Razorwing starfighter that destroyed the Void Core.

After Caldris was captured on Velhaven, Solvane took command of the Stormhawk once more to search for him. In 4 AVR, following Caldris's rescue, Solvane flew the ship at the Battle of Velnar, where it entered the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and aided in its destruction from within. Over the next three decades, the ship fell out of Caldris's hands and was stolen by a succession of criminals and thieves, ending up in the ownership of the trader Unkar Plutt on Drava, a remote world in the western half of the galaxy. The Stormhawk was eventually stolen by the Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger Sefa, former Crimson Pact voidtrooper Tane, and Defiance mechan DD-5 to escape the forces of the Crimson Pact in 34 AVR. Shortly thereafter, it was reunited with Caldris and Wrentak.

Following Caldris's death soon after, the Stormhawk became a Defiance vessel piloted by its new owner, Sefa, throughout much of the Crimson Pact-Defiance War. In 35 AVR, the ship was once again captained by Solvane during the Battle of Vauldor, which saw a decisive victory for the Defiance and Sephari Order, before being returned to Sefa, who had been newly dubbed a Drellos.

## Description

The Stormhawk was a YT-1300 light freighter of the YT-1300f variety. Its basic shape was defined by two convex saucers welded together, a pair of front-facing mandibles and an outrigger-style, side-mounted cockpit with transparisteel viewports. Apart from the shape of its chassis, the Stormhawk retained very little of its off-the-dock instrumentation. Because its successive owners either upgraded or downgraded every system according to their personal specifications, the freighter ended up becoming a unique ship. These customizations, however, came at the price of reliability, and the Stormhawk was known to suffer frequent breakdowns in the heat of battle. After the circular military-grade sensor dish was lost during the Battle of Velnar, the dish was replaced with the base rectangular model which was originally supplied with the ship. During the Battle of Saldreth, the Stormhawk again lost its rectenna, and it was replaced with a military-grade rectenna similar to that of the one lost during the Battle of Velnar. Additionally, the Stormhawk had a dejarik table, which was installed by Dav Solvane when upgrading the ship, featuring creatures found throughout the galaxy for the amusement of passengers and crew.
