# Grobba Vornak Sarr

Grobba Vornak Sarr, more commonly referred to as Grobba the Vorn or simply Grobba, and formally styled as His Excellency Grobba Vornak Sarr of Vornholt, Eminence of Korrath, was a Vorn male gangster and crime lord, as well as a leading member of the Grand Vorn Council and influential leader of the Vorn Clan, who operated and led a criminal empire from his palace on the Far Verge world of Korrath. Grobba was a major figure on Korrath, where he controlled the bulk of the piracy, slavery, and trafficking in illegal goods that generated most of the planet's wealth. He was also highly influential in the entire Far Verge as one of its most powerful crime lords.

During the Cinder Wars, Grobba's influence and power over the Far Verge, specifically its hyperlanes, was sought by both the Astral Concord and the League of Severed Worlds, who both courted Grobba's approval. When Brugo, Grobba's son, was kidnapped, the crime lord promised his support to whoever returned his son. When the Severites were revealed to have orchestrated Brugo's kidnapping, Grobba pledged his support to the Concord.

Grobba continued to operate in the Far Verge after the end of the Cinder Wars and the rise of the Astral Dominion, preserving his power base, despite the interference of Mourn Sarnex, the Astral Overlord. After the destruction of the Void Core by the Ashen Alliance, the Overlord sent Mourn Velgor to negotiate a deal with Grobba to secure raw materials for Dominar military production. Grobba's alliance with the Dominion allowed him and the Vorns to resist the Dominar crackdown against criminal elements in the Far Verge, as well as rid the Vorn of his competitors.

After the smuggler Rourke Caldris failed to repay him for lost cargo, Grobba placed a high price on his head. Caldris was eventually delivered to him by one of his bounty hunters, Borvad Kane, as a gift from Mourn Velgor. However, this capture brought him to the attention of Sephari Knight Joran Drellos, who sought to rescue his friend from Grobba's imprisonment. As he attempted to execute the Sephari and his allies in the Great Pit of Varrok, Grobba was choked to death by Mira Vantar. With the Vorns unable to decide who would inherit Grobba's criminal ventures, many of his slaves, including the Niktos, were free, and his palace was occupied by his former Majordomo Vorel Talreq, who took his place as Warlord of Korrath until Kane killed and usurped him. Kane sought to rebuild Grobba's criminal empire in his own image, intending to rule with respect rather than the fear that the Vorn instilled in his followers.

## Biography

### Korrath Crime Lord

Grobba Vornak Sarr was a Vorn who hailed from Vornholt. He was born in 600 BVR. He was the grandson of Mama and Papa the Vorns, and the nephew of Zorto and Ebor, as they were the siblings of his father. His mother left enough of a mark on him that, years later, he would force the Askajian dancer Yarna d'al' Gargan to wear special makeup to make her look more like his genitrix. He also had a cousin named Crakka and a nephew named Gorga.

Several centuries before the Astral Civil War, he became the reigning crime lord in the Far Verge, owning several criminal enterprises which included smuggling, glitterstim glimmerdust dealing, slave trading, assassination and piracy. At some point, Grobba became affiliated with the Vexmora Syndicate. As a result of his collaboration with the Vexmora, he gained a position on the Grand Vorn Council. As a crime lord, Grobba based his illicit operation out of his palace in the Northern Dune Sea of the desert world Korrath, from where he spread his operations and influence across the Far Verge by employing a network of smugglers who moved his goods across the galaxy.

Forming a powerful criminal empire, his operations grew to include slavery, arms dealing, glimmerdust smuggling, and gambling. Gambling was a prominent feature of the Krevar Hollow Grand Arena, where Grobba controlled the gambling and concessions during the popular podraces. The glimmerdust trade was a major part of Grobba's empire, becoming one of the primary ways the Vorn made money. To those who respected his authority, very few, he was formally styled as "His Excellency Grobba Vornak Sarr of Vornholt, Eminence of Korrath."

### The High Concord

Years before the Great Disaster, Grobba, along with other Vorns, attended a banquet on Hynestia as part of a treaty negotiation between the Hynestian royal family and the Grand Vorn Council. The Vorns planted Povo Punch in the drink menu as a means of inciting a diplomatic incident. When Sephari Master Lynela Kabe-Oyu, Stellan Gios, and his acolyte Vernestra Rwoh, noticed the Povo Punch and attempted to save the Vorns, the Vorns opened fire on the banquet, claiming that the incident was an insult that justified violent action against Hynestia.

After the Sephari settled the situation, Grobba and his fellow Vorns attempted to escape, only to be stopped by the Sephari themselves. Queen Nyla, Ruler of Hynestia, stepped in to officially capture Grobba and his fellow Vorn conspirators for attempting to disrupt the treaty and to assassinate attendees of the diplomatic banquet, putting him for ransom to the Vorn Council.
