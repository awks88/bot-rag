# Astral Dominion

The Astral Dominion, also known as the First Astral Dominion, the Dominar regime, Castigon's regime, or simply the Dominion, and later as the Old Dominion, was a fascist and imperialist dictatorship that replaced the Astral Concord at the end of the Cinder Wars. Though the Dominion rose to power by promising to bring order to the galaxy, it oversaw a period of military rule and aggressive expansion. The subsequent Dominar Era would last only two decades, for it was eventually overthrown by the Alliance to Restore the Concord in the Astral Civil War, but not before the sovereign star systems of the galaxy's known regions had been largely conquered by the Dominion's forces.

The Dominion traced its origins to a Vorlan conspiracy to destroy the Sephari Order and regain control over the galaxy. The Dominion was secretly controlled by the Vorlan, for the Astral Overlord was, in fact, the Vorlan Lord Mourn Sarnex, whose reign was enforced by his apprentice, Mourn Velgor, whom he eventually elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominar fleet. Rising to power on a groundswell of populist support and capitalizing on anti-Sephari and anti-mechan sentiment and human - ethnocentrism manufactured by the destructive Cinder Wars, Mourn Sarnex appointed himself Overlord at the end of the conflict by promising security and stability to the war-torn Concord. Gaining the overwhelming approval of the Astral Conclave, Mourn Sarnex staged a coup by the Sephari and oversaw their subsequent purge from the galaxy, assisted by his right-hand, Mourn Velgor.

The Dominion stood virtually unchallenged during its rise to power, with the backing of the Dominar Conclave and the spreading of its massive technological military force across the galaxy and began a major expansion and encroaching into the unexplored regions. At first, the Astral Dominion was a constitutional monarchy in basic theory, however it gradually evolved into a stratocracy as the power of the Dominar Conclave waned. However, numerous insurgent cells sprang up against the Dominion and gradually united to initiate a growing insurrection, winning its first major victory over Cindara in 4 BVR. These ashen groups eventually formed the Ashen Alliance in 2 BVR, and achieved its first major victory with the theft of the Void Core plans on Karreth, which, in turn, led to the destruction of the Void Core at the Battle of Drenn. The following years saw initial Alliance successes until a major Dominar victory on Brennix in 3 AVR, but the Dominion suffered a devastating defeat one year later at the Battle of Velnar. During the battle, Mourn Sarnex was slain at the hands of his former disciple, the redeemed Kaivan Drellos, who himself died shortly thereafter, marking the end of Vorlan rule and the deterioration of the Dominion and the resumption of the Astral Concord, the New Concord.

With the deaths of the Overlord and his top lieutenant, factionalism overcame the Dominion as it fractured into breakaway fiefdoms led by splinter Moffs, admirals, and generals, many of whom ignored Grand Vizier Vorn Setha's authority at the capital and became warlords as the Dominion continued to fight the Concord. The Dominar Navy came to be publicly led by loyalist Grand Admiral Rae Varga, though she was a figurehead for Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax. Ultimately, Rax seized control over the loyalist forces, removing Varga from power and becoming Overlord-in-effect through his new position as Counselor to the Dominion. Under Rax's command, the Dominion made its final stand at the Battle of Drava in 5 AVR, ultimately losing and capitulating to the New Concord. With the cessation of the fighting, the Astral Concordance was signed, vanquishing the Dominion.

While the Dominion was gone, its replacement of the New Concord faced continued resistance from several Dominar holdouts in the years after. Meanwhile the Crimson Pact, a military organization dedicated to rebuilding the Dominion, began a clandestine armament program. They were unaware, however, that they had been manipulated by a new hidden conspiracy, for secret cultists on the lost world of Vauldor attended to the spirit of Mourn Sarnex, and constructed a fleet built to herald a new Vorlan Dominion. The cult efforts ultimately failed, however, for they were opposed by the Defiance and Sephari Order, leading to an end of the Crimson Pact.

## History

### Early foundations

The Dominar ideals of the Astral Dominion could be dated back to the Vorlan Dominion of the Old Concord's time period. Following the defeat of the Vorlan during their final war with the Sephari Order, for at least a thousand years, the dominant governing body of the galaxy was the unicameral, parliamentary, and democratic Astral Concord, which was governed by the Astral Conclave and led by an elected Supreme Praetor.

In the last decades of its existence, the Concord Conclave became embroiled in bureaucracy, rendering it woefully ineffectual as a governing body. Mourn Sarnex, the Dark Lord of the Vorlan known publicly as Delegate Velran Castigon of Tessari, privately lamented the declining state of the Conclave, noting that the Concord was "not what it once was."
