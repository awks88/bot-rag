# Veyron Dask

Veyron Dask, a Synth Flux-sensitive human male, was a venerable if maverick Sephari Master who lived during the last years of the Concord Era. He was a wise and well-respected member of the Sephari Order, and was offered a seat on the Sephari Council, but chose to reject and follow his own path. Adhering to a philosophy centered around the Living Synth Flux, Dask strove to follow the will of the Synth Flux even when his actions conflicted with the wishes of the High Council. After encountering Kaivan Drellos, Dask brought him to the Sephari Temple on Velarion, convinced he had found the Fated One. His dying wish was for Drellos to become a Sephari and ultimately restore balance to the Synth Flux.

Born on the Core World Velarion, the capital of the Astral Concord around 80 BVR, Dask learned the ways of the Synth Flux as the Acolyte of Sephari Master Maren before ultimately attaining the rank of Sephari Knight in around 58 BVR. In 44 BVR he would go on to take an apprentice of his own, Orven Taskorr, who adhered to the principles of the Sephari Code more than his own master. Although their relationship was sometimes tough, the two Sephari overcame early difficulties during a mission to Pijal, which concluded in Dask turning down a request to join the Sephari Council to continue teaching his apprentice. When the Commerce League initiated a blockade of the Mid Verge planet Tessari in 32 BVR, Dask and Taskorr were dispatched to resolve the crisis at the request of Supreme Praetor Halvor Calvex. Influenced by the mysterious Vorlan Lord Mourn Sarnex, the Combine launched a full-scale invasion of Tessari, forcing the Sephari to rescue Queen Sennra Velora after the fall of Aldria.

During their journey to the galactic capital, the group was forced to land on the Far Verge planet Korrath where Dask encountered a young slave named Kaivan Drellos. Using a scientific method to gauge the boy's potential, he learned that Drellos had more synth-nodes in his blood than any Synth Flux-sensitive in galactic history, including Grand Master Oodran. The revelation that he was the result of a miracle birth—a child born of a mother, but had no father—confirmed Dask's suspicion that Drellos was the Fated One destined to restore balance to the Synth Flux. Before departing the desert world with Drellos, Dask was confronted by Sarnex' Vorlan apprentice Mourn Threnn. Although their duel was brief, it convinced Dask that the Vorlan had returned after a millennium of silence.

At the Temple, Dask was adamant that Drellos was the Fated One and should, therefore, be inducted into the Sephari Order. However, having inspected Drellos for themselves, the High Council deemed him too old, emotional, and dangerously full of fear and anger. Dask saw their decision as a temporary setback, but the need to resolve the situation on Queen Velora's homeworld forced him to set aside his plans for Drellos. During the Battle of Tessari, Dask and Taskorr confronted Mourn Threnn together. While they initially managed to fend off the Vorlan Lord's attacks, master and apprentice were eventually separated during the plasma-brand duel, resulting in Dask sustaining a mortal injury. Rushing to his master's aid, Taskorr fought and ultimately defeated the Vaelmorni Vorlan, but could not save Dask. With his last breath, Dask made Taskorr promise that he would take Drellos as his apprentice. Having secured Taskorr's word, Dask became one with the Synth Flux, dying with the certainty that he had found the prophesied Fated One.

## Biography

### Early life

Veyron Dask was a male human born on the galactic capital of Velarion circa 80 BVR. He was taken to the Sephari Temple as a boy for training by the Sephari Order and was assigned to the Heliost Clan where he learned plasma-brand combat from Sephari Master Tera Sinube.

When Dask was twelve years old, he was assigned as a Acolyte to Sephari Master Maren, from whom he learned the ways of the Synth Flux. He'd spent his last night in the novices' crèche laughing with his friends, imagining all the adventures he would have as a Acolyte, and practicing with his plasma-brand in the sparring room until Master Vossa ordered him to bed.

When the future Sephari Master Eno Cordova first joined the Sephari Order, he became close friends with Dask, who helped Cordova learn about and get used to Velarion.

### Apprentice to Maren

Around 68 BVR, when he presented himself to his new Master in Maren's quarters, Maren observed that Dask was frightened. After a moment of silence, and with pressing from Maren, Dask confessed that he was. When asked why, after looking into himself through the fear, Dask answered that he was not afraid of Maren, but of not becoming a Sephari, that he was afraid of failing and of not being worthy. Maren then summarized it as Dask being afraid of himself, of a future not being the one he wanted. Dask confirmed it, fearing that Maren would reject him for his cowardice. Maren instead said that it was very wise, surprising Dask. Smiling, Maren explained that most Acolytes would deny their fear, and even if they admitted it, they would not have Dask's self-knowledge. Maren further praised Dask for his honesty, insight, and intelligence.
