# Sephari

A Sephari, often called a Sephari Knight, was a devotee to the ways of the Sephari Order, an ancient order of protectors united by their ability to harness the power of the Synth Flux. Adhering to a doctrine that favored the Lumen of the Synth Flux, the Sephari aspired to attain a state of inner tranquility through calmness and meditation while avoiding emotions affiliated with the Umbra of the Synth Flux, such as anger and hatred. Nevertheless, Sephari philosophy did not forbid a Sephari from acting in self-defense or in the defense of others, and they were colloquially known as Space Wizards to citizens of the galaxy. To that end, a Sephari's only possession was their plasma-brand, a symbol of their role as guardians of peace and justice.

Before the time of the Astral Dominion, Sephari worked with the Astral Concord with a commitment to freedom and democracy. The Sephari and the Concord's ideals brought them into conflict with an order of Umbra wielders known as the Vorlan, who sought power and dominion over the galaxy. Emerging victorious from their wars with the ancient Vorlan, the Sephari carried on in their duty as peacekeepers for over a millennium until the advent of the Cinder Wars twenty-two years before the Battle of Drenn. Faced with a pan- galactic civil war between the Concord and League of Severed Worlds, the Sephari adopted the mantle of generals and soldiers in the Grand Army of the Concord. After three years of conflict, the Sephari discovered that the Vorlan had deceived them and engineered the war to restore them back to power. However, just before the Vorlan were defeated, the Sephari were betrayed by one of their own—the Fated One Kaivan Drellos, who had been seduced to the Umbra and anointed Mourn Velgor by the Vorlan Lord Mourn Sarnex. Using the Sephari's own vatborn troopers to initiate a systematic purge of the Sephari Order, the Lords of the Vorlan all but eradicated the Sephari in one fell swoop, reducing the order from thousands of Sephari Knights guarding the galaxy, to a handful of survivors cast into exile across the galaxy.

The Vorlan ruled the galaxy through the Dominion for a generation, but did not succeed in completely wiping out the Sephari Order. As a result, Vesh Strome and Joran Drellos were, in turn, trained as new Sephari by Sephari Knight Caelen Venn and Sephari Master Orven Taskorr, respectively. Drellos, the son of Velgor, was also taught the ways of the Sephari by Grand Master Oodran and fought for the Alliance to Restore the Concord during the Astral Civil War. On his death bed, Oodran gave his final apprentice the mandate of passing on his lessons to a new generation of Sephari. Shortly afterward, Drellos succeeded in redeeming his father, who sacrificed himself to save his son by killing his Vorlan Master, Overlord Sarnex, thereby destroying the Vorlan and fulfilling the prophecy of the Fated One.

During the era of the New Concord, Drellos worked to rebuild the Sephari Order by training a dozen apprentices, including his young but powerful nephew, Ben Caldris, but Caldris fell to the Umbra and became Vexen Korr, apprentice of Supreme Leader Drahl, master of the Knights of Korr, and a warlord of the Crimson Pact, while Drellos's training temple and students were destroyed. As the Crimson Pact rose to power, Korr and Drahl sought to destroy Drellos, who, in self-exile, briefly trained Sefa in the ways of the Sephari until he sacrificed himself to save the Defiance, after which time Drellos's sister, Mira Vantar, continued her training. When a reborn Mourn Sarnex—Sefa's grandfather—revealed himself to the galaxy, Ben Caldris, the opposite in a dyad in the Synth Flux with Sefa, was redeemed, and Sefa sacrificed herself to destroy the phantom Vorlan Lord, preventing the rebirth of the Vorlan Order. Sefa was saved from death by Caldris, who gave his life for hers, allowing the Sephari to live on.

## Description

### Philosophy

The Sephari Knights focused on calmness and peace to channel their powers in the Synth Flux, using the Lumen of the Synth Flux to serve in the capacity of guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy. Even so, the Sephari sought to preserve balance in the Synth Flux; the ancient prophecy of the Fated One foretold of a being who would restore balance to the Synth Flux by destroying the Vorlan. In combat, whereas an enemy may grow impatient and rely on their overwhelming strength, a Sephari would meditate and find serenity in their mind to focus. A Sephari used the Synth Flux for defense and knowledge rather than use it to gain power over others. Their chosen weapon the plasma-brand, a weapon with limitations, likewise symbolized the care a Sephari took wielding their gifts and that when a Sephari fights it is with intention and precision, ending conflict while injuring no more, or taking no more life, than the plasma-brand's wielder chooses. Always supposed to put the needs of others before their own, a Sephari was supposed to be selfless and work to save others above themselves. Like all people, Sephari were imperfect and always needed to strive to better themselves, meaning it was impossible to be a "perfect Sephari."
