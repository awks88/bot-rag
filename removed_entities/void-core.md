# Void Core

A Void Core was a gargantuan space station armed with a planet-destroying superlaser powered by voidstone crystals created by Grand Warden Verrin Vorlock.

## Death Stars

### DS-1 Battle Station

The DS-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, also known as the DS-1 Orbital Battle Station, was a superweapon that was originally designed by the Karnaki during the waning years of the Astral Concord. The project was later taken over by the Astral Dominion; however, the plans for the battle station were stolen from Karreth by the Ashen Alliance, which led to the weapon's destruction at the Battle of Drenn, but not before it unleashed its planet-destroying cannon on the Dominar security complex on Karreth, Valdha City on Valdha, and annihilated the entire planet of Aurelia.

The name "Void Core" was coined by the scientists who found themselves working on the project, such as Galen Walton Erso. The name was adopted by the Dominion at large, with Major Lio Partagaz of the Dominar Security Bureau considering it "a prescient turn of phrase."

### DS-2 Battle Station

After the destruction of the first Void Core, the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station was the second and final Void Core constructed by the Astral Dominion and was designed to be more powerful than the original. During the Battle of Velnar, the Void Core II's energy shield was destroyed and the Ashen used the unfinished superstructure of the battle station against it, destroying the weapon as they had its predecessor.
