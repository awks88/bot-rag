# Oodran

Oodran was a legendary Sephari Grand Master who led the Sephari Order through the time of the High Concord, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Vorlan, and during the transformation of the Astral Concord into the Astral Dominion. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Oodran trained generations of Sephari, ultimately serving as the Sephari Order's Grand Master. He played integral roles in defending the Concord during the Cinder Wars, survived Directive Null, and lived to pass on the Sephari tradition to Joran Drellos, and unlocking the path to immortality.

Born in 896 BVR, Oodran served the Sephari Order for centuries, becoming Sephari Grand Master and helping to teach many novices. During the High Concord Era, in 382 BVR, Oodran helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Sephari and the anti-Sephari Path of the Open Hand cult. After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow, he decided to keep the living weapons against Synth Flux-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless, out of the Sephari Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Sephari High Council to train Acolytes aboard the Star Hopper, leading them and other Sephari in fighting the Riven pirate organization in 232 BVR. After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Oodran reappeared in 230 BVR on Veltar, where he helped his former Acolyte Kantam Sy and other Sephari battle the Riven, accompanied by a former Sephari who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Azlin Rell. As the Riven had also weaponized the Nameless, Oodran hoped that Rell's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Sephari find a way to fight them.

Oodran was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Concord. A leading member of the Sephari High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Dovan Krell and Kel-Adan-Voran. During the Invasion of Tessari, in 32 BVR, the maverick Veyron Dask introduced Kaivan Drellos to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Fated One. The Sephari elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Drellos too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Sephari Code. After Dask's death during the liberation of Tessari, the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Oodran's continued opposition to Drellos's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Sephari discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Vorlan, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two, Oodran was convinced that at least one more Vorlan Lord remained active following Orven Taskorr's victory over the Vorlan apprentice Mourn Threnn.

Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems—led by Count Maren, Oodran's one-time Acolyte—threatening to secede from the Concord by 22 BVR. Unable to defend the entire Concord against this betrayal on their own, the Sephari took command of the newly-formed Grand Army of the Concord, with Oodran himself leading an army of vatborn troopers against the Severite Mechan Army of the League of Severed Worlds in the first battle of the Cinder Wars, rescuing the Sephari and Delegate from execution. For three years Oodran led the Concord military's war effort as a Sephari General, determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Veyron Dask, who helped Oodran learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Oodran's efforts against the Severites were undermined by Mourn Sarnex, the Dark Lord of the Vorlan who conspired to destroy the Sephari and restore the Vorlan to power. After the Great Sephari Purge commenced in 19 BVR, killing thousands of Sephari both on Velarion and across the galaxy, Oodran confronted the self-declared Astral Overlord but failed to cut short Sarnex' reign and consequently retreated into exile, leaving the Vorlan Master to consolidate his power with a new apprentice, the former Kaivan Drellos-turned- Mourn Velgor, at his side.

Oodran's remaining years were spent living in isolation on the remote world of Mourndahl. After the death of Orven Taskorr, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Oodran to train Kaivan's son Joran Drellos. Oodran initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the galaxy as better off without him and refused Taskorr's request. After being convinced by Taskorr to enter the Cave of Evil, in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Joran Drellos, who soon after sought out the legendary Grand Master in the hope of becoming a Sephari Knight. He trained Drellos for a time, until despite Oodran's warnings Drellos hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 AVR, with his health rapidly declining, Oodran died of old age when Drellos returned to see him. He became one with the Synth Flux, shortly before Joran redeemed his father Kaivan, who fulfilled his destiny as the Fated One by killing Sarnex.
