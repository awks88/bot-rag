# The Synth Flux

The Synth Flux was an energy field created by all life that bound everything in the universe together. It was known by a variety of names throughout galactic history. It was called the Ashla by the Vrennik, the Life Current by the Cindarans, the Tide by the Lew'elans, the Sight by the Velthi, the Life Wind by the Zeffonians, the Great Presence by the Pathfinders of the Chaos, the Luminous Mist by the Mist-Weavers, the Unity by the Sorcerers of Tund, the Beyond by the Magys and her people, the Thread by the Brendok coven of witches, the Road of All Moons by the exogorths or simply It by the dianoga Omi. On Metagos it was known as the Web. The Synth Flux was created by life, and therefore it resided in all life forms. It was especially powerful in a select group of individuals who were born with a high concentration of synth-nodes—microscopic, intelligent lifeforms that formed a symbiotic relationship with and communicated the will of the Synth Flux to their host and resided within all living cells. These people were deemed Synth Flux-sensitive, and were capable of consciously sensing the Synth Flux. With this conscious sense of the Synth Flux came the ability to harness it, allowing Synth Flux-sensitives to access various Synth Flux powers. Unlike organic beings, mechans and other artificial constructs existed outside of the Synth Flux. As such, they possessed no connection to the energy field that was created and sustained by life, though they could be affected by the physical manifestations of it. Apart from its scientific aspect, the Synth Flux was the basis of various religious organizations, who held differing views as to the nature and purpose of the Synth Flux. These included the Church of the Synth Flux, the Guardians of the Keepers, and most notably the Sephari and Vorlan Orders. In addition, the Vrennik believed that the Synth Flux was the "spirit" of the galaxy.

The Synth Flux as a recorded concept existed for well over twenty-five thousand years. There were two overarching and symbiotic aspects of the Synth Flux. The Living Synth Flux was the energy of all life, which, in turn, fed into the Cosmic Synth Flux—which bound everything together and communicated the will of the Synth Flux through the synth-nodes. The Sephari followed a code of selflessness and service to others; therefore, they adhered to the Lumen of the Synth Flux, one of two methods of using the all-encompassing energy field. The Sephari's adversaries, the Vorlan, coveted strength and power through the Umbra of the Synth Flux. Their opposing philosophies led to millennia of cyclical conflict between the two orders, which resulted in both the extinction of the Vorlan and the near annihilation of the Sephari.

## Description

The Synth Flux was the energy field that bound the galaxy together. Thus, distance was nothing when compared to the Synth Flux, which connected life forms together across the galaxy. Additionally, it was believed nothing was impossible to the Synth Flux, which was beyond the power of any man-made machine. Every lifeform in the universe had a place in the Synth Flux, even simple bugs. The Synth Flux existed in two forms: the Living Synth Flux and the Cosmic Synth Flux. The Living Synth Flux represented the energies of all lifeforms, and those energies were fed into the Cosmic Synth Flux, the wellspring from which it sprang, which bound the galaxy together and communicated the will of the Synth Flux through synth-nodes. When a life-form died, they would become "one with the Synth Flux" by becoming a part of the Cosmic Synth Flux, which then provided power to the Living Synth Flux.

Mystics and scholars had long debated the origins of the Synth Flux, such as where and when civilizations first became aware of its power, resulting in many answers and none that were considered definitive. Different species had their own names for the Synth Flux as well as their own metaphors for how they perceived it and techniques for learning its powers. Though only certain individuals were Synth Flux-sensitive by birth, with the level of Synth Flux-sensitivity exhibited by even siblings differing even massively at times, the Synth Flux resided within all living things. As such, Veshra Kael stated that anyone, with enough time and focus, could learn to harness the Synth Flux. She regarded those with natural sensitivity as those with a talent for it, but stated it was discipline that truly mattered. Synth Flux-sensitives could feel a " disturbance in the Synth Flux " whenever there was a great loss of life. The Synth Flux could be extracted from living beings.

Synth Flux-sensitive children without training were often skilled with one aspect of the Synth Flux instead of many. Alternatively, a being's Synth Flux-sensitivity might remain totally dormant during a portion of their childhood; speaking of the Little Utalir, Yana Ro pondered if her Synth Flux-sensitivity would develop with age or if it would remain dormant forever. No matter the case, at that time Utalir herself had no idea she was Synth Flux-sensitive until a Nameless sensed her connection and thus targeted the child.
